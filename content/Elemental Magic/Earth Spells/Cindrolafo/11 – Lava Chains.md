---
title: 11 – Lava Chains
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== restrain with lava  
The caster causes chains of molten metal to burst from the ground and reach to shackle the target. If they fail the resistance check, they are Rooted and take 3d6 fire damage at the start of each of their turns while Rooted by this spell. The target can make a 5d6 vs STR from the chains after being shackled at the end of each of their turns, ending the spell on a success. If the target is flying or can fly, they are grounded for the duration of the spell.
