---
title: 2 – Liquify
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== debuff (slowed)  
Liquify turns a horizontal surface of earth, sand, or clay into a viscous mud. The creatures in the area are slowed to quarter normal movement, and their Move defense values are reduced by -2.When fully finessed, any prone creatures in the area need to spend their standard action to stand up regardless of their armor type.
