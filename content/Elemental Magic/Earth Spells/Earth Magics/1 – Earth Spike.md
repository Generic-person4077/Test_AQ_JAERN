---
title: 1 – Earth Spike
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== earth damage  
The target is hit with a large spike made from stone that does 1d6 + 1d6/2F earth damage.Alternatively the spike remains in the caster’s hand as a 10 pound rock.
