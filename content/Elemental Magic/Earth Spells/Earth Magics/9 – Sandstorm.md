---
title: 9 – Sandstorm
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PER negates  
==Target:== LOS 80 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== debuff (blinded)  
The caster creates a raging sandstorm to appear in the area. Creatures moving through the sandstorm or starting their round inside must make a 5d6 vs PER or be blinded for the round.Additionally, creatures that end their turn in the sandstorm take 2d6 earth damage as they are buffeted by the sands.
