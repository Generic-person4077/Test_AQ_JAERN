---
title: 15 – Terovoki
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 100 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== summon earth elementals  
Terovoki summons 4 + 2/F earth elementals to any point within the caster’s range. A source of earth must be available and must be part of the planet.Refer to Appendix D: Spell Clarification for creature write ups and control mechanics.
