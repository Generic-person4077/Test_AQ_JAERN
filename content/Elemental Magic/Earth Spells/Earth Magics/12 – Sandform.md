---
title: 12 – Sandform
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== become sand  
Sandform turns the target and their possessions into grains of fine sand. The target is capable of movement, can slip under doors or through cracks and receives a +6 bonus to magic DVs.
