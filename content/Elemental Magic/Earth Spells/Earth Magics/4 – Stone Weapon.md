---
title: 4 – Stone Weapon
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 fifth  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single weapon  
==Effect:== create earthen weapon  
This spell enables the caster to gather existing earth and stone into any manual weapon (table found in Chapter 5: Equipment) and wield it without a STR requirement for the spell’s duration. The weapon does its base damage as earth damage and counts as magical for bypassing resistances. The weapon is enchanted to +1/F. The weapon can be handed to other creatures, but regains its normal STR requirement.
