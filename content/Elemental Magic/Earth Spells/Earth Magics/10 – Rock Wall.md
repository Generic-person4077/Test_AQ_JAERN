---
title: 10 – Rock Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 10 x 120 + 30/F’  
==Effect:== stone structure  
Rock Wall causes a 10’ tall 120 + 30/F’ long wall rise from the ground in front of or away from the caster. Alternatively it can create a horizontal bridge of the same dimensions.The structure crumbles if it takes 120 points of damage or at the end of the duration.
