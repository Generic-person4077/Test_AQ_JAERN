---
title: 11 – Catapult
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== throw large stones  
Catapult allows the caster to hurl large stones as a standard action (including the round the spell is casted) at creatures up to 100’ away with a ranged attack using the higher between +12 and their normal ranged modifier. On hit, the stone deals 4d10 blunt damage (core). Critical multiplier is capped at x3. Hits against knocked prone creatures are always critical.
