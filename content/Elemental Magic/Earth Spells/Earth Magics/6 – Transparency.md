---
title: 6 – Transparency
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + .5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== see through stone  
By this magic, the caster allows light to pass through earth or stone, creating a cylindrical viewing portal. This portal allows viewing in only one direction of the caster’s choice.
