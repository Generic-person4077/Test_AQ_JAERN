---
title: 5 – Wax or Wane
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== debuff  
Choose between Wax and Wane when casting this spell.Wax grants Exceptional STR to its target as well as raising damage from weapon attacks by 2 + 1/F (bonus).Wane grants Inferior STR to its target as well as reducing damage from weapon attacks by 2 + 1/F (bonus).
