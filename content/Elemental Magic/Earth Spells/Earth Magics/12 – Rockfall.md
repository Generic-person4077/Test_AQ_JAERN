---
title: 12 – Rockfall
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI halves*  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 rounds  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== rocks fall!  
Caster stomps the ground and sends rocks and debris flying in the air. For the duration the debris rain down in the area, dealing 6d6 earth damage to anyone passing through. The affected creatures must use their fifth to attempt the resist check or they automatically fail.
