---
title: 3 – Trench
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 10 + 3/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 5 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== dig hole in earth  
A caster using Trench can dig a hole into the earth, sand or clay of the indicated volume. The dimensions are as the caster desires, within the given area of effect. The trench is created by displacing the earth and remains until manually filled.When fully finessed the spell affects stone.
