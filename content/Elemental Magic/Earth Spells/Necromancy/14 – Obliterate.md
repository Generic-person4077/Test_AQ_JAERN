---
title: 14 – Obliterate
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== utter annihilation  
The target of Obliterate must be touching earth, stone, metal, or some other earthen substance. If they fail the resistance check, they are immediately, irrevocably turned to dust.
