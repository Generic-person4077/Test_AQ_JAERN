---
title: 11 – Toughen Sinew
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target(s)  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F undead creatures  
==Effect:== grant DR to undead  
This spell reduces the damage taken by the target from all attacks and spells by 2 + 1/2F points for the duration.
