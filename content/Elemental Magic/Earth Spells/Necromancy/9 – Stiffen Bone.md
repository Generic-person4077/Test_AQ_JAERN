title: 9 – Stiffen Bone
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target(s)  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F undead creatures  
==Effect:== armor undead  
The target's magic DVs are all increased by 2 + 1/F for the duration of the spell.
