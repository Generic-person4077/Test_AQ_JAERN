---
title: 15 – Theft of Opportunity
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== cheat death  
The caster must sacrifice a living creature during the casting of this spell.  The caster gains its chance at life as a second chance. The next time the caster dies, or would be destroyed if the caster is undead, the caster is restored to half DP and automatically regains consciousness. Any bleeding or organ loss that would cause death again is healed.This spell has no effect if the caster already has another Theft of Opportunity active on themself, or if they have any other Second Chance effect active.
