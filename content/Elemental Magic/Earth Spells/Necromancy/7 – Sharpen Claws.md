---
title: 7 – Sharpen Claws
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target(s)  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F undead creatures  
==Effect:== increase damage  
The target(s)'s natural attacks are increased to deal 1d(8+2/F). This also enchants the attacks so they count as magical for the purposes of bypassing Magic or Silver shield or similar protections.
