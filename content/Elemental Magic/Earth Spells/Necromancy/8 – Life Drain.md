---
title: 8 – Life Drain
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F living creatures  
==Effect:== drain life  
The target(s) of this spell suffer 4d6 points of blight damage, and the caster heals for the total damage done up to their maximum DP. A bright white glow leaves the target and is absorbed into the caster when this is cast.
