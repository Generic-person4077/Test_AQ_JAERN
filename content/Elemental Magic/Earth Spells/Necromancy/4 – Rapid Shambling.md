---
title: 4 – Rapid Shambling
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target(s)  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F undead creatures  
==Effect:== make undead faster  
This spell gives the target(s) 10 + 5/F feet of extra movement per round, in their primary form of movement.
