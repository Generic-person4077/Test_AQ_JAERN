---
title: 2 – Minor Life Drain
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single living creature  
==Effect:== siphon life  
The target suffers 1d6 points of blight damage and the caster heals for the same amount as damage up to their maximum DP. A dim white glow leaves the target and is absorbed into the caster when this is cast.
