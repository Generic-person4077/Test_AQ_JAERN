---
title: 14 – Mankiller
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== until tripped  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ square  
==Effect:== make lethal trap  
Mankiller creates a trap that uses swinging swords, thrusting spears, etc. to deal death and destruction. The setup and type of weapons are up to the caster. The trap is so elegant that it cannot be discovered until it’s too late. The victim dies unless the resistance check is made.
