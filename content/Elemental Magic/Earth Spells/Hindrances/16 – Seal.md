---
title: 16 – Seal
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== special*  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single door  
==Effect:== special*  
A Sealed area cannot be passed. In addition, if the caster finesses the effect, anyone who touches the door must succeed at a resistance check of five dice against PWR or suffer the associated effect:1 Finesse: Magical Paralysis2 Finesses: Petrifaction3 Finesses: Death4 Finesses: Body Destruction
