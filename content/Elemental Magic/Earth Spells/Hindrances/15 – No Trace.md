---
title: 15 – No Trace
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PER*  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== touched beings  
==Effect:== leave no trace  
At the end of the spell, the caster and those they touch during the casting time may travel and leave no visible signs of their passage, regardless of the terrain. Anyone making the resistance check can find the trail, but only one attempt per seeker is allowed.
