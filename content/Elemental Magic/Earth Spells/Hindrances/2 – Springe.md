---
title: 2 – Springe
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER*  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== until tripped  
==Area:== 5’ square  
==Effect:== lay a snare  
Springe enables the magician to quickly lay a snare, the type that uses a bent sapling or other springy object to tighten a rope around a victim’s feet and lift them into the air. The snare can be found and harmlessly tripped by making the resistance check.
