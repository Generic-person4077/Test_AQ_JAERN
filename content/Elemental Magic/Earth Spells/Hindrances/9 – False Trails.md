---
title: 9 – False Trails
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER*  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F’ minutes  
==Area:== touched beings  
==Effect:== obscure travel  
False Trails enables the caster and those they touch within the casting time to leave signs of passage in 2 + 1/F directions as they travel. One trail is real, of course, but can only be positively identified by making the resistance check. These trails do not radiate magic, and dissipate at the end of the duration.
