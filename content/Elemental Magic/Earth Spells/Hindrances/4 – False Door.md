---
title: 4 – False Door
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER*  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single surface  
==Effect:== create fake door  
False Door creates a door that leads nowhere and cannot be opened. Upon close examination and a successful resistance check, it becomes apparent that the door is fake. False Door counts as a door for the purposes of other Hindrances spells.
