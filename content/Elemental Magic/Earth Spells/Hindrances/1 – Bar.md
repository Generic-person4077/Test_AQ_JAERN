---
title: 1 – Bar
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== single door  
==Effect:== bar a door  
Bar applies a magical force across the inside of an existing door just as if it were barred with a stout length of wood. STR attribute checks to open the door are made with one additional die.
