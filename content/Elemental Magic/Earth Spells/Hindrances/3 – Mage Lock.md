---
title: 3 – Mage Lock
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== single door or chest  
==Effect:== creates trap  
The door or chest that this spell is cast on deals 3d6 pure magical damage to the person that moves it in any way. The target of this spell has a rune on it that, if known, will give it away that there is a spell on the object. Once activated, the rune is dispelled.
