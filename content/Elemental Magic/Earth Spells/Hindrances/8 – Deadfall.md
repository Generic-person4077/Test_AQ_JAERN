---
title: 8 – Deadfall
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER*  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== until tripped  
==Area:== 5’ square  
==Effect:== create deadfall  
Deadfall enables the caster to identify a good trap area and speedily set up a deadfall trap using available materials. The trap can be noticed by making the indicated resistance check and then tripped without damage. Stumbling into a deadfall inflicts 6d6 points of magical, blunt damage.
