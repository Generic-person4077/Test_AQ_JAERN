---
title: 6 – Caltrops
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F’ minutes  
==Area:== special  
==Effect:== prickly footing  
Caltrops will create hundreds of four-headed spikes, one of which always points up. The caster chooses 2 + 1/F horizontal squares that are 5’ x 5’ to be affected by this spell. Each square must be contiguous with another square.  Moving through the area must be done at quarter normal movement rate or suffer 4d6 points of magical, piercing damage per round.
