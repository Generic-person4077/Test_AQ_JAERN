---
title: 10 – Mantle’s Protection
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 round  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== create shield  
The caster creates a shield of molten metal in front of the target. This shield temporarily grants the target +7 to their magic DVs and 5 ranks of Damage Resistance until their next round of action.
