---
title: 5 – Temper
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== heat/cool metal  
Temper allows the caster to rapidly super heat and/or cool a piece of metal. This is hot and rapid enough to temper metals in weapons and armor. Anyone holding the superheated metal may choose to drop the metal with no consequence or, without proper safety equipment, will suffer 3d6 of fire/heat damage for up to 4 rounds if they continue to hold it.
