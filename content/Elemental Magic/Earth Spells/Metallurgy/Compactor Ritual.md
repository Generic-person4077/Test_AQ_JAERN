---
title: Compactor Ritual
---
This ritual takes 1 hour to complete and can be performed by any mage with 12 ranks of [[Elemental Magic/Earth Spells/Metallurgy/|Metallurgy]]. The ritual allows a single touched creation marked with the caster’s Signature Design to compress and uncompress from a 1” cube. A compressed object weighs ¼ of a pound.  

If it leaves the possession of the caster, it regains the weight of the original, uncompressed form at the end of the round. This weight cannot cause any damage.  

The gold cost of this ritual varies depending on the activation time for compressing and expanding the object:  


| Cost      | Activation Time |
| --------- | --------------- |
| 100 Gold  | 1 action        |
| 500 Gold  | 1 fifth         |
| 1000 Gold | 1 reaction      |

A creature cannot have more Compactors than their non-modified PWR attribute.
