---
title: 2 – Call of Inspiration
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F days  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== call finished piece  
This spell is cast when holding a finished piece made by the caster. This allows the caster to call out to the other finished works they have marked with Signature Design in the form of vibrations, light glowing, and, if in LOS, summon them to their creator. The item moves through the air at a rate of 60’+10/F feet per round. If the item is not free-standing, the item can still be drawn to the caster. (GM Discretion).
