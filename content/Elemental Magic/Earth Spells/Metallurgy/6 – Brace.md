---
title: 6 – Brace
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== fortify constitution  
In preparation to endure extreme conditions the caster fortifies the target. The target gains Exceptional HEA. For every two finesses, the target also gains a rank of Resist Fire.
