---
title: 4 – Expert Hands
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== toughen appendage  
This spell allows the caster to harden their bones, skin and muscles in the hand up to the elbow similar to the properties of steel. This allows the caster to use their hands in place of their tools to handle hot and/or sharp materials without injury.
