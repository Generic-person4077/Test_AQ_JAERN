---
title: 15 – Magnetic Terrain
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs STR*  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minute  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== magnetize ground  
The caster magnetizes the ground, pulling any ferromagnetic objects to it. Creatures wielding ferromagnetic weapons must make a resistance check of 6d6 vs STR each round to successfully attack during the duration; those wearing ferromagnetic armor must make 6d6 vs STR or be forced prone and Magically Paralyzed, repeating the resist check each round; ferromagnetic projectiles from missile and linear weapons fall to the ground, never reaching their targets.
