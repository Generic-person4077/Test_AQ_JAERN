---
title: 6 – Deflect Missile
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== protect from projectiles  
Deflect Missile gives the target the ability to cause any missile with a ferromagnetic head to swerve away to one side of the target. It is possible that bystanders can be struck by the missiles instead (GM discretion).
