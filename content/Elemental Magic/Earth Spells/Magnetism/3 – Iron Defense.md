---
title: 3 – Iron Defense
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== increase defense  
The magician uses Iron Defense to temporarily add a minute amount of the earth’s magnetic power to their body, increasing one magic DV of their choice (combat, ranged,or grapple) by 4 + 1/F.
