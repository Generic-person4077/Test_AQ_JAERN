---
title: 4 – Modify Permeability
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch, multitouch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F sets of armor  
==Effect:== remove PWR penalty  
Modify Permeability changes the magic permeability of a set of armor, so that it no longer attracts magic. This removes the PWR resist penalty from a set of armor. Magical armors make a 4d6 vs PWR check to negate this effect. By finessing this spell, the magician can affect up to 3 sets of armor, as long as they touch them during the casting time.
