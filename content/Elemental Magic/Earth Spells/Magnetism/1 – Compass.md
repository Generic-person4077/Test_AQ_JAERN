---
title: 1 – Compass
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== find magnetic north  
Compass enables the magician themself to existing magnetic fields around them. For the duration of the spell, they can sense magnetic north (not necessarily true north).
