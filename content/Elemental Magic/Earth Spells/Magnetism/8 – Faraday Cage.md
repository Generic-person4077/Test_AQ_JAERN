---
title: 8 – Faraday Cage
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single set of armor  
==Effect:== ground electromagnetism  
This spell magically grounds a set of ferromagnetic armor so that it grants the wearer 1 + 1/2F ranks of Resist Lightning.
