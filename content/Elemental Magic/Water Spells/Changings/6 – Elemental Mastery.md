---
title: 6 – Elemental Mastery
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F’ cube  
==Effect:== change element  
Elemental Mastery enables the magician to convert the indicated volume of one element (fire, air, water, or earth) to another. Element is transformed to the next element in following order:Earth → Fire → Air → Water/Ice → EarthFor every 2 finesses the element is transformed one step further.
