---
title: 8 – Disguise
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== change appearance  
Disguise allows the caster to alter the appearance of the target to within 10% of the same height and weight. Gender, hair, and eye color, etc. may be freely changed. This does not change any creature abilities or racial abilities, and changes are purely aesthetic.
