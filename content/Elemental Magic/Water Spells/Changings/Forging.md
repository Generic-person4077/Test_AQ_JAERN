---
title: Forging
---
A quick ritual can be performed to turn raw materials into finished objects (small statues, tools, weapons, etc). The workmanship cannot exceed what the caster is capable of without the use of this ritual and all skill checks need to be attempted.  

The ritual takes 1 minute to complete and affects up to 25 lbs of touched material. Once complete the objects are mundane and will retain their shape. The type of materials that can be affected is determined by the caster’s highest rank between [[Earth Magics/]], [[Metallurgy/]] and [[Changings/]] group:  

| Rank | Material              |
| ---- | --------------------- |
| 1    | Clay, Glass, Aluminum |
| 2    | Gold                  |
| 3    | Silver                |
| 4    | Tin                   |
| 5    | Platinum              |
| 6    | Copper                |
| 7    | Brass                 |
| 8    | Bronze                |
| 9    | Iron                  |
| 10   | Steel                 |
| 11   | Adamantite            |
| 12   | Exotic Metals         |
