---
title: 7 – Malform
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== impose disability  
The caster uses Malform to temporarily reshape the target into a less efficient form. They can choose one Inferior Attribute, Susceptibility or Disability to bestow upon the creature for the duration. A creature can be affected multiple times, but granted disability must be different between castings.
