---
title: 2 – Blade Oil
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F weapons  
==Effect:== increase damage  
Blade Oil covers the weapon in a sticky corrosive substance. Targeted weapons deal extra 1d4 acid damage on every hit.This spell can be combined with Damage Alteration to change its damage type, with acid damage replacing water or cold in the rotation.
