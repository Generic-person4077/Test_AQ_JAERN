---
title: 3 – Mend Flesh
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== heal  
The target of Mend Flesh heals 3d6 damage points. For every two finesses, one limb/organ has its functions restored if damaged within the last hour.
