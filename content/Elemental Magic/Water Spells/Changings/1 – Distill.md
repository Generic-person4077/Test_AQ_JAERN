---
title: 1 – Distill
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 2 + 1/F pints  
==Effect:== change liquid  
Distill allows the caster to change any aqueous liquid into another aqueous liquid. The magical properties of the liquid (if any) are unaltered. Magical liquids that succeed on a resistance check of 3d6 vs PWR are not affected. The caster needs to have tasted the created liquid, which must be non-poisonous and non-magical.
