---
title: 14 – Mutate
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== “improve” species  
Mutate empowers the caster to make changes or “improvements” to a species. Extra arms or legs can be added or removed, eye position and number changed, skeletal shape altered, etc. One change can be made with the base spell, plus one change for every two finesses.Detailed Mutation mechanics and a table of creature abilities are provided in Appendix E.The use of this spell should be recorded by the GM similarly to a created magic item.
