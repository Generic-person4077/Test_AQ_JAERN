---
title: 10 – Augment
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== temporary improvement  
For the duration of the spell the caster makes 1 + 1/2F changes to the creature following Mutation mechanics. At the end of duration any experience or HEA points used to pay for the changes are refunded. A creature can only have one instance of this effect active.Detailed Mutation mechanics and a table of creature abilities are provided in Appendix E.
