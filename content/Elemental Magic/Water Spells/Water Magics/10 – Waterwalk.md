---
title: 10 – Waterwalk
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== touched creatures  
==Effect:== walk on water  
Waterwolk allows the caster and anyone he can touch during the casting time the ability to walk on water or any other liquid surface (such as mud, acid, quicksand, or lava) without sinking as if it were harmless solid ground.If this spell is cast on submerged creatures, the spell carries them to the surface at a rate of 60’ per round, and protects them from any sort of depressurization effects.
