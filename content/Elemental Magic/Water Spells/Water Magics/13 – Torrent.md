---
title: 13 – Torrent
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 2000 + 1000/F’ radius  
==Effect:== torrential downpour  
Torrent causes the sky to cloud over and unleash extreme flooding rain for the duration. Such a massive downpour of rain may cause structures to be damaged or even washed away (GM’s discretion).
