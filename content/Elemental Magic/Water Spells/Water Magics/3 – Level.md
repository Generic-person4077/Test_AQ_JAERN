---
title: 3 – Level
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== raise/lower water level  
Level allows the caster to control the level of any aqueous liquid up or down 4 + 2/F’ within the area indicated. The liquid is displaced, not created or destroyed.
