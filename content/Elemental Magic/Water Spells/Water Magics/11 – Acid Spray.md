---
title: 11 – Acid Spray
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 25’ x 50’ cone  
==Effect:== spray acid  
The caster gathers elemental water and acidifies it, before blasting it in a 25’ x 50’ cone in front of them. Those in the area must make the 5d6 vs AGI resist check to halve 6d6 acid damage. Those that fail the resistance check are additionally covered in acid, and will take 3d6 more acid damage at the end of their next turn, unless they take an action to wipe the acid off.
