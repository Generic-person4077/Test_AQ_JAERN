---
title: 8 – Hail
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== blast of hail  
Hail causes a blast of hail to strike the area indicated within range causing 2d6 cold damage to all within the area. The caster can choose a different area within range each round the spell is active for the hail to blast.
