---
title: 14 – Divert
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== 100 + 50/F’ wide  
==Effect:== change course  
Divert enables the caster to change the course of a stream or river up to 100 + 50/F’ wide, redirecting the flow in any direction, even uphill. Using this spell to make a new channel is permanent, but directional changes (reversing normal flow direction, causing the river to stand still, etc.) will only last one day.
