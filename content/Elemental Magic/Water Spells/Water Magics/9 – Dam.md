---
title: 9 – Dam
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 100 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 60 + 15/F’ radius  
==Effect:== stop water flow  
Dam creates an invisible wall of force which stops the flow of water in a stream or currents in a lake or sea. If the dam can touch land at both ends, it will hold back rising water. If land cannot be touched the water will flow around the Dam with increased force on either side. When the spell ends, any pent-up water is released.
