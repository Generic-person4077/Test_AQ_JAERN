---
title: 2 – Well
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 6 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== dig a well  
Well enables the caster to find potable water in any natural environment. They cause a 6 + 2/F’ radius area of natural ground to muddle and form a pool of potable drinking water. The water is real and can be stored for later use.
