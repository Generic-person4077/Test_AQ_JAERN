---
title: 7 – Akvoblovo
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 10’ x 80’ cone  
==Effect:== high pressure water  
Akvoblovo opens a small opening to the elemental plane of water, from which torrents forth a blast of high pressure water in a cone. All in the area of effect must make the resist check to halve 6d6 water damage.
