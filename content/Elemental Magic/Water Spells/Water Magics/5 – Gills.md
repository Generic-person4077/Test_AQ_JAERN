---
title: 5 – Gills
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== grant waterbreathing  
Gills enables the recipient to extract oxygen from water. This spell grants the target(s) the Water Breathing creature ability for the duration.
