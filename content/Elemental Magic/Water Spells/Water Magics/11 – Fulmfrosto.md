---
title: 11 – Fulmfrosto
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== destruction  
Fulmfrosto causes latent moisture within the area of effect to suddenly freeze, totally collapsing and destroying most wooden or other porous objects (such as sandstone). Living creatures must make a 5d6 vs HEA check to halve 8d6 ice/cold damage.
