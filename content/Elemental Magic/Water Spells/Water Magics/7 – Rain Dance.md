---
title: 7 – Rain Dance
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 2000 + 1000/F’ radius  
==Effect:== control precipitation  
The mage who performs a Rain Dance can cause a heavy rain or heavy snow (depending on prevailing air temperature) to begin falling, or if cast during a rain or snowstorm, cause the precipitation to stop. The area is centered on the caster, and will move with them.
