---
title: 1 – Spray
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== saturate  
Spray causes elemental water to spurt from the caster’s fingertips. The spray is very fine, and everyone in the area of effect who fails the resistance check is Drenched as if in a thunderstorm. Torch-sized fires are extinguished, and items susceptible to water damage might be harmed (GM discretion).
