title: 15 – Akvovoki

==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== water elementals  
Akvovoki summons 4 + 2/F water elementals from any water source (a cup of water is sufficient) within the target area.Refer to Appendix D: Spell Clarification for creature write ups and control mechanics.
