---
title: 3 – Poison Sight
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== reveal poisons  
Poisonward reveals the presence of poisonous creatures or items in the target’s LOS. Such creatures or objects are highlighted with a green aura in the target’s vision.  If the spell is finessed four times, the target additionally gains one rank of Resist Poison for the spell’s duration.
