---
title: 14 – Ward of Obfuscation
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PER negates  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== up to 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== obfuscate items  
The caster sets Ward of Obfuscation by touching at least four corners of the area during the casting time while designating 1 + 1/F inanimate items in the area. At the end of the casting time, those items become invisible, and can only be seen from within the area by creatures making the indicated resistance check. Any creature that enters the area makes the resist check, but are unaware that they are making it if they fail.
