---
title: 16 – Multi Ward
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== one ward  
==Effect:== make ward stronger  
Multi Ward is cast on another warding spell cast by the caster. It forces an additional 1 + 1/F resistance check(s) to be made to breach the ward. The duration and area of the ward is not extended by this spell.Multi Ward can only be cast on any single ward once.
