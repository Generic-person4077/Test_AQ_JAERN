---
title: 11 – Magic Ward
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== special*  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== up to 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== shield against spells  
The magician must touch at least four corners of the area to be warded during the casting time. If a spell is cast and targeted into the warded area, the opposing caster must roll a number of dice equal to the rank of the spell. The caster of this ward then rolls 11 dice. If the opposing caster’s roll is higher, the spell functions normally, otherwise, the spell does not function but the units for casting the spell are still spent.
