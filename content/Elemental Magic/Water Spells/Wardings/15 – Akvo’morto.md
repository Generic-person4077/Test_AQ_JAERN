---
title: 15 – Akvo’morto
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== up to 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== lethal ward  
The caster sets Akvo’morto by touching at least four corners of the area during the casting time. If the resistance check is failed the creature’s lungs fill with poisonous water, causing death in four rounds if the drowning and poison is not remedied.
