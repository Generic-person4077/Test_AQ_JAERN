---
title: 6 – Ward of Alertness
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== willing targets  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== touched targets  
==Effect:== proof against ambush  
The caster touches multiple willing targets during the casting time, and for the spell’s duration, the touched individuals are protected from ambushes. The touched individuals automatically win the contested roll if they are being ambushed.
