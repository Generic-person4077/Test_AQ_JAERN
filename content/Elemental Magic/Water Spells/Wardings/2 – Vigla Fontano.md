---
title: 2 – Vigla Fontano
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== fountain alarm  
Fountain can be cast on a source of water (a cup of water is sufficient). When an intruder enters the area, the water starts to bubble with a gentle burbling noise that awakens the caster, even from a magical sleep.
