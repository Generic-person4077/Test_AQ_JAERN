---
title: 4 – Stay
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== up to 5 + 3/F’ radius  
==Effect:== prevent exit  
The magician must touch at least four corners of the area to be warded during the casting time. Anyone can enter the warded area, but cannot leave unless the resistance check is made. The ward extends 10 + 5/F’ above the ground, and is capped above.
