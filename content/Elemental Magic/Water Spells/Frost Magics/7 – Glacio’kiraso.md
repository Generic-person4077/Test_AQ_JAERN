---
title: 7 – Glacio’kiraso
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 rounds  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== frost armor  
The target is granted armor of chilling frost. All that engage in melee combat against the target take (1 + 1/4F)d6 of cold damage and lose 5+5/2F’ of movement speed per strike on the target. This loss lasts 3 rounds.
