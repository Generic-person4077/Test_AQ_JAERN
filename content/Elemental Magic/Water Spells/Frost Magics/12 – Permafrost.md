---
title: 12 – Permafrost
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== encase in ice  
The caster freezes a target. They are Magically Paralyzed while encased in the ice. The ice has a Damage Point pool of 50. Upon hitting 0, the ice shatters and the target is unfrozen.
