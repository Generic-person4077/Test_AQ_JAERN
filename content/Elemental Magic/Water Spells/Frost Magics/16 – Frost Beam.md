---
title: 16 – Frost Beam
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 100’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== freezing beam  
The caster fires a beam of ice that deals 10d6 cold damage in a 10’ wide line to a target. Then at that point, in a 20 + 5/F’ radius, an explosion of 14d6 cold damage occurs. The resist check is applied for both sources of damages, so only 1 roll is necessary. A creature in the overlapping area only takes 14d6 cold damage, not 24d6.
