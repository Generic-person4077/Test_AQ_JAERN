---
title: 10 – Ice Lance
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 80 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 4 + 1/F’ wide line  
==Effect:== spear of ice  
The caster launches a giant spear of ice from their hands, dealing 9d6 of cold damage in a 4 + 1/F’ wide line between them and the target point. Any creature that fails the resist check also loses 1/5 of their movement speed for 3 rounds.
