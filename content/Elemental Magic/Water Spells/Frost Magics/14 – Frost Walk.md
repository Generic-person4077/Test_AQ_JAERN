---
title: 14 – Frost Walk
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== touched creatures  
==Effect:== walk on air  
Caster enchants the beings that they touch. The targets can freeze the air at the bottom of their feet, allowing them to move about in such a way that they can walk on air. If they are knocked prone while they are in the air, they can cause the air beneath them to freeze and they will remain at their current altitude.
