---
title: 9 – Chilly Wind
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 100’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== susceptibility to cold  
The caster blows a gust of cold air at the target(s), those that fail are chilled by the cold air and gain 1 + 1/2F ranks of Susceptible to Cold.
