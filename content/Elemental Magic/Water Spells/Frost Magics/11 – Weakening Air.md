---
title: 11 – Weakening Air
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 2 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== weaken foes  
The caster sends a blast of chilling air. The target creatures gain Inferior STR, HEA or AGI for the duration. For every two finesses, grant a rank of a different Inferior Attribute.
