---
title: 4 – Frost Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 5’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 5’ x 7’ x 2”  
==Effect:== wall of ice  
The caster creates a wall of ice in front of them, blocking or creating an obstacle. The wall is translucent, LOS spells may be targeted through it with a 2d6 Arcana check.The wall has 10 + 5/2F DP and can be destroyed with 4d6 vs STR check.
