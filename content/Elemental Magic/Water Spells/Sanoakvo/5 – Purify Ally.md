---
title: 5 – Purify Ally
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 fifth  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== buff, utility  
If an ally has been subjected to a negative status effect, this spell allows them to reattempt the resist check one die easier. The following status effects are immune to this spell: Paralyzed, Magical; Suspended in Time/Blue-Fielded; Prone
