---
title: 13 – Sanafluctus
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 25 x 50’ cone  
==Effect:== healing  
All chosen creatures within the area of effect are healed 5d8 DP. This healing can go over the targets’ maximum, and any overflow becomes temporary DP.
