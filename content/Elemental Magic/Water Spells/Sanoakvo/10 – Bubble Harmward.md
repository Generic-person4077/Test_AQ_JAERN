---
title: 10 – Bubble Harmward
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== defense  
A bubble of magical water surrounds the target of this spell. Until the bubble pops, the target has 2 ranks of Resist Fire. The bubble will pop when the spell ends or when the target takes over 15 damage at once, in which case it will halve the damage of the triggering attack before popping.
