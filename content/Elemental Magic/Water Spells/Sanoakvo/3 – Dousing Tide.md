---
title: 3 – Dousing Tide
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== buff  
A swirling thread of magical water dances around the target for the duration. Until the spell ends, the target gains 1 rank of Resist Fire and cannot suffer from the Burning condition.
