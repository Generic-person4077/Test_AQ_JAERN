---
title: 12 – Vestments of the Nereid
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== buff, healing  
When this spell is cast, a spectral cloak of mystical water and swimming fish surrounds the caster. For the duration of the spell, all healing performed by spells cast by the target gains a flat bonus equal to ¼ of the caster’s PWR.
