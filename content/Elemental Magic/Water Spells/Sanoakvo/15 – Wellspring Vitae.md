---
title: 15 – Wellspring Vitae
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 10 + 2/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== healing  
The target of this spell is healed DP equal to (caster’s PWR)d8. This healing can go over the target’s maximum, and any overflow becomes temporary DP.
