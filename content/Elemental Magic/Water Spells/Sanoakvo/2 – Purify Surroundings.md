---
title: 2 – Purify Surroundings
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== utility  
This spell purifies an indicated volume of any aqueous liquid, making the water safe to drink. Certain magical sources of pollution or corruption may be able to resist this effect; in this case, it must make the resist check.
