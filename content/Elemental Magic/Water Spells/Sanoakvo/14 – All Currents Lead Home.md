---
title: 14 – All Currents Lead Home
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== buff, healing  
This spell calls the currents of elemental water to guide a recently departed soul back to its body and restore it to life.Refer to Appendix D: Spell Clarifications for general resurrection mechanics.
