---
title: 11 – Tides’ Rejuvenation
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== buff  
The target of this spell must be someone who has suffered an Attribute drain within the last 24 hours. After the spell is finished, a single Attribute drained is restored to them. This spell does not regenerate stressed Attributes or Exceptionals.
