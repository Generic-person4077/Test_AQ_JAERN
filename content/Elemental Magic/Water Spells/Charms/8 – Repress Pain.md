---
title: 8 – Repress Pain
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== represses pain  
The targets of this spell that are willing or fail the resistance check have their pain tolerance increased significantly for the duration of the spell. They gain two ranks of Resist Pain. Additionally, they also receive 1 point of damage reduction from any source.
