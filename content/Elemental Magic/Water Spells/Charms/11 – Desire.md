---
title: 11 – Desire
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== instill desire  
All targets that fail the spell’s resistance check have a certain desire that is stated by the caster at the time of the spell's casting. For the duration, the targets will seek to satisfy this desire. Targets cannot be made to harm or kill themselves with this spell. After the duration expires, the target will be confused and vaguely remember events that transpired while enspelled.
