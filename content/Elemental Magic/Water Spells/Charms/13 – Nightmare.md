---
title: 13 – Nightmare
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== withdraws mind  
The target of this spell has their mind thrust into their subconscious, where they have neverending and terrifying nightmares. Their body seems vacant and vegetative and are Unconscious.
