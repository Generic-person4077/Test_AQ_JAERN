---
title: 14 – Bewitch
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 month  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== impose control  
This spell functions identically to Betask, except the duration is greatly increased, and the target lose 3 points of WIL if they choose to disobey the caster.
