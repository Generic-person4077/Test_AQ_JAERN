---
title: 10 – Betask
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== impose will  
Betask enchants any creature to do the caster’s bidding if they fail the resist check. The target is charmed. If the target chooses to disobey the caster, the spell is broken, but the target loses one point of WIL permanently. When the spell ceases, the target will be completely aware of the enchantment and their actions while enspelled.
