---
title: 2 – Rockabye
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== put creature to sleep  
The target of this spell slips into a natural sleep and becomes Unconscious, and can be woken by another creature using their action to jostle the sleeping creature awake. Any loud noises or other stimuli can awaken the target as well. If the target of the spell is in combat, the resist check becomes a 3d6 vs WIL check.
