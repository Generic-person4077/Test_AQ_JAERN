---
title: 16 – Chaotic Battlefield
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 60 + 15/F’ radius  
==Effect:== attack friends  
Chaotic Battlefield warps the minds of creatures hostile to the caster, distorting their perception of friend and foe. For the duration the affected creatures treat the caster and the caster’s party as friends and direct their hostile actions towards their own allies. At the end of each round they may attempt the resistance again to break the effect.
