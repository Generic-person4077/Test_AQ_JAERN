---
title: 12 – The Drowning
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== “drown” creatures  
A target that fails this spell’s resistance check believes they are drowning and will start suffocating. Targets do not actually have to breathe for the spell to function, but creatures that must breathe will drop Unconscious and begin dying after a number of rounds equal to their HEA score. While a creature is gasping for breath, they are Stunned and cannot perform any other action. The target(s) of this spell may reattempt the resist check at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success.
