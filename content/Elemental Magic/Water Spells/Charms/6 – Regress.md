---
title: 6 – Regress
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== remember  
While casting this spell, the caster concentrates on a specific time period or memory that he wishes the target to recall. If the target is willing, they can recall the memories as if they just occurred. This spell can be used to see through mental blocks, but not through active spell effects. If this spell is finessed for effects, it can conjure up an additional memory per finesse.
