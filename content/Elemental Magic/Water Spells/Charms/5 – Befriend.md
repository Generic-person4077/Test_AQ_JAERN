---
title: 5 – Befriend
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs CSE negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== enemies to friends  
Befriend immediately cancels any hatred towards the caster and their companions felt by the targets of the spell. This will last as long as none of the perceived friends take a hostile action (including casting spells) towards the Befriended targets. If the targets of this spell are engaged in combat with the caster and their companions, the resist check is 3d6 vs CSE.
