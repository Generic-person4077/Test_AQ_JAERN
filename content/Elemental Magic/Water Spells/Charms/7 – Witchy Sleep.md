---
title: 7 – Witchy Sleep
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== induce magical sleep  
If the targets of Witchy Sleep fail their resistance checks, they slip into a deep, magical sleep and become Unconscious. They will only awaken when the spell is canceled by the caster, Revoked, the duration expires or the target takes more than 10 points of damage.
