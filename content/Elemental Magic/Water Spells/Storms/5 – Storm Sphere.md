---
title: 5 – Storm Sphere
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== sphere of lightning  
Gathering power from the elemental planes of water and air, the caster generates a swirling storm of water and lightning. The sphere is a 5’ radius, and any creatures that begin their turn in the sphere must make a 3d6 vs AGI to halve 3d6 water damage.As a fifth, the caster can cause an arc of lightning to strike at a creature within melee range of the sphere, and they must make a 3d6 vs AGI to halve 3d6 lightning damage.As an action, the caster can move the sphere up to 30 feet in any direction.
