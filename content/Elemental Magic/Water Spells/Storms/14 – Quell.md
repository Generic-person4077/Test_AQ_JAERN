---
title: 14 – Quell
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 2000’ radius  
==Effect:== quell storm  
Quell allows the caster to calm any natural storm, and acts as a rank 14 Revoke on any magical storm. The skies calm, winds die, and rain stops. Any environmental hazards or storm-based abilities are negated by this.
