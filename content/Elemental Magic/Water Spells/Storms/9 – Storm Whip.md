---
title: 9 – Storm Whip
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== storm retribution  
As a reaction to being hit with a combat attack, the caster lashes out with a whip of crackling lightning and water. The target creature must make a 4d6 vs AGI to halve 3d6 lightning damage plus 3d6 water damage. If they fail the resistance check, they are also Drenched.
