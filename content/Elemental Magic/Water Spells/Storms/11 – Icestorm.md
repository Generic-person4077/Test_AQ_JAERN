---
title: 11 – Icestorm
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minute  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== call hailstorm  
This spell must be cast outside, and has no effect if it is already storming.The caster summons powerful winds, rain, and invokes chilling temperatures in the target area. Each creature that ends their turn within the area must make a 5d6 vs AGI check to halve 2d6 cold/ice damage plus 2d6 water damage.
