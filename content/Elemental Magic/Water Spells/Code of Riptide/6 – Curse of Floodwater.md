---
title: 6 – Curse of Floodwater
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== difficult terrain  
The caster fires a water jet at the ground, forming viscous pools of water and flooding the area. This lowers the speed of any creatures in the target area (excluding the caster) by 1/3 of its original value and grants an Inferior AGI when they are inside the area.
