---
title: 2 – Wellspring
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs STR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== knock off balance  
The caster stomps one foot and generates a small geyser to erupt under the chosen target. If the target fails a 3d6 vs STR they are knocked prone. The geyser is 10’ high, and originates from the ground.
