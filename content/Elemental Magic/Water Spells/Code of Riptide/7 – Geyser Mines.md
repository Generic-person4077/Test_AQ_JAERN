---
title: 7 – Geyser Mines
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI partial*  
==Target:== LOS 100’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 4 + 1/2F targets  
==Effect:== more geysers  
The caster stomps their foot after channeling, causing 4 geysers to erupt on the battlefield under and around enemies. Any enemy hit by a geyser must succeed a 4d6 vs AGI or be blasted 10 feet away from the caster and be knocked prone. Succeeding at the check will blast the target 10 feet away but they will not be knocked prone. Any target can only be hit once per round by this spell. Each geyser is 15’ high, and originates from the ground.
