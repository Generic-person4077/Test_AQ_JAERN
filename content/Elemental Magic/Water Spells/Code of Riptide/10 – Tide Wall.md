---
title: 10 – Tide Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none*  
==Target:== LOS 100’  
==Duration:== 4 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== 7’ tall x 5’ long x 2’ thick  
==Effect:== wall of solid water  
The caster channels for 2 rounds, then summons a wall of turbulent water at a chosen location. The water appears to be falling in a motion similar to a waterfall, but stays within the confines of its dimensions; this rushing motion prevents any attempt to see through the wall. It is possible to move through the wall, taking 2d6 pain damage if they have been hit by edged or piercing damage within the last round (this will apply Drenched) but LOS targeting through the wall is not possible. If the caster chooses, they can impose a 4d6 vs STR check to pass through the wall.
