---
title: 12 – Roaring Current
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 60’  
==Duration:== 4 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F targets  
==Effect:== seashell effect  
The caster charms the target such that all they can hear is the sound of intensely crashing surf; they can still be Deafened by spells such as Thunderclap. This makes normal communication with them very difficult and they have to be shouted at. The targets also cannot complete any actions that take over one round.
