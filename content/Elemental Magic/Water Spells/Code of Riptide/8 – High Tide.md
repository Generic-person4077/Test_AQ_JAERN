---
title: 8 – High Tide
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== LOS 100 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== Instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== more salt in more wounds  
The caster generates a prism of salty water and fires it at chosen target, dealing 4d6 pain damage (halved for resist) if they have been hit by edged or piercing damage within the last round.  If no edged or piercing damage was taken, the target automatically succeeds the resist check but they are still Drenched.
