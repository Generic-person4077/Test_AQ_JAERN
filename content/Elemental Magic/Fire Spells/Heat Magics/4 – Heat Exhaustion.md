---
title: 4 – Heat Exhaustion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minute  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== expose to heat  
The caster channels heat from the elemental plane of fire towards the target, affecting it with symptoms of acute heat exhaustion. If the target fails the resist check, they are Dazed as they are overcome with nausea and dizziness. The target reattempts the resist check at the end of each of its rounds, ending the spell on a success.
