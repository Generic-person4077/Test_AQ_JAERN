---
title: 16 – Temperature Adaptation
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 year  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== enhance homeostasis  
The caster attunes and magically tethers their body with the elemental plane of fire, using it as a thermal source or sink to enhance their own body’s homeostasis. During the duration of the spell, the caster can use an action to adapt their body to the temperature around them, allowing them to survive and negate the effects of hypo- or hyperthermia in any temperature between -60 °F and 200 °F.
