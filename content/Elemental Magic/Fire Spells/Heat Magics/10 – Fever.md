---
title: 10 – Fever
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA partial*  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== raise body temperature  
Fever induces a dangerously high fever in the target’s body. The target makes the resist check at the start of each of their turns. If the target fails, they take 2d6 heat damage and are Dazed for the turn. If they succeed, they take 1d6 heat damage and can act as normal.
