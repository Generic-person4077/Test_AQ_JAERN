---
title: 13 – Heat Stroke
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs HEA*  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== kill with heat  
The caster channels lethal heat from the elemental plane of fire towards the target. If the target fails the resist check, they take 13d6 heat damage and fall Unconscious as they are overcome with heat exhaustion. If the target succeeds, they take half damage and do not fall Unconscious but are still affected by heat exhaustion, and are dizzy and sluggish. For the duration, the target is Delayed.
