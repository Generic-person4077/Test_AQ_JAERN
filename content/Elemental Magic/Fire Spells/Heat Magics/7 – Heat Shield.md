---
title: 7 – Heat Shield
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F creatures  
==Effect:== protect from heat  
The caster creates a translucent, insulating shield around the target with a reaction when the target would take heat damage. The shield absorbs 10 + 5/F points of heat damage, and dissipates immediately after. If the heat damage would exceed the DP of the shield, the excess damage carries over to the target.
