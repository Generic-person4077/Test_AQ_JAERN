---
title: 5 – Arcane Scorch
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== burn spell units  
Arcane Scorch forcibly burns the target’s magical units, causing them to magically scorch the target. The target loses 3 + 1/F magical units, and takes the same number of units lost in d6 of pure magical damage. If the target has multiple pools of units, determine which pool this spell burns from randomly. If this spell burns through all the units remaining in a pool, it does not carry over to other pools.
