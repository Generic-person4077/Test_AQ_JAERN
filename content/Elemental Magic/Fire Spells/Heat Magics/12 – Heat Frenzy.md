---
title: 12 – Heat Frenzy
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs CSE*  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== Hot-blooded rage  
Heat Frenzy causes the target to be overcome with hot-blooded rage. The target makes the resist check at the start of each of their turns. If the target fails, they take 3d6 heat damage and become enraged, and must attack the closest creature to itself, be it friend or foe. If the target succeeds the resist check, they take 2d6 heat damage and can act as normal.
