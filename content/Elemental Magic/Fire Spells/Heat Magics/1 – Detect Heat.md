---
title: 1 – Detect Heat
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== 120 + 30/F’  
==Effect:== detect hot things  
The caster can detect anything within the spell’s area that is hotter than 150 °F. The caster simply knows the location of all hot objects.
