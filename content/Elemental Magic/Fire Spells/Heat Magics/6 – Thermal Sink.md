---
title: 6 – Thermal Sink
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== heat sink  
The caster touches an object and magically links it with the elemental plane of fire. The object then becomes a heat sink, which can absorb any excess heat and channel it to the elemental plane of fire. The object becomes cool to the touch, and is immune to any spells or effects that would heat it up. With 4 finesses, the caster can target a single creature with this spell.
