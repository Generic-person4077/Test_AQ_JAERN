---
title: 8 – Hut of Warmth
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== warm resting area  
Hut of Warmth creates a 10’ radius immobile dome. The spell ends early if the caster leaves the radius. Up to 6 creatures (of humanoid size) can fit inside the dome with the caster, and each finesse for area allows 2 more creatures. The atmosphere inside the dome is warm, dry, and comfortable, regardless of the weather outside.
