---
title: 9 – Heat Syncope
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA partial*  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== cause fainting  
The caster channels torrential heat from the elemental plane of fire towards the target. If the target fails the resist check, they fall Unconscious as they are overcome with heat exhaustion. If the target succeeds, they do not fall Unconscious but are still affected by heat exhaustion, and are dizzy and sluggish. For the duration, the target is Delayed.
