---
title: 16 – Consumir Chamas
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== turn target to ash  
If the target fails the resistance check, they are engulfed in a black flame. The flame consumes the target, killing the target and turning its body to ash.
