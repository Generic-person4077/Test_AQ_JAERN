---
title: 3 – Blast Shield
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== resist explosions  
The caster creates a translucent, shimmering shield around their body. They gain 1 + 1/2F ranks of Resist Explosives, which functions like other resist abilities, but applies to resist checks and damage from explosions of grenades and combustible objects.
