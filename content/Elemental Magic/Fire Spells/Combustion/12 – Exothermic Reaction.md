---
title: 12 – Exothermic Reaction
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single explosion  
==Effect:== explode again  
The caster casts this spell on an explosion they can see within the range, which causes the explosion to explode again, although with a weaker effect. The resist check for this secondary explosion is reduced by 1 die, and the damage is reduced by 2 dice. Any additional effects beyond damage are not replicated.
