---
title: 1 – Detect Combustibles
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== 120 + 30/F’ radius  
==Effect:== detect combustibles  
The caster can detect flammable and combustible objects in the spell’s area. Flammable objects are outlined in orange, and combustible objects are outlined in red.
