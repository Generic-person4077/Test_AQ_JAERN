---
title: 4 – Abey Combustion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/2F rounds  
==Area:== single explosion  
==Effect:== delay explosion  
This spell is cast on an explosion the caster can see within range. The caster creates a magical sphere of force that surrounds the explosion within a 1’ sphere, which will keep the explosion in stasis until this spell ends. Once the spell ends, the explosion occurs as normal.
