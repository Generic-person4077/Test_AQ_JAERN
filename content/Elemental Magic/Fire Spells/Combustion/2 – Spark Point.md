---
title: 2 – Spark Point
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F lb. object  
==Effect:== makes object combustible  
Spark Point allows the caster to make any non-magical flammable item (wood, paper, cloth, etc.) combustible. With two finesses, the caster can target non-magical, non-flammable objects as well. With four finesses, the caster can target magical items, which must make a 4d6 vs PWR to negate this effect. This spell is most useful as a preparatory step to other Combustion spells.
