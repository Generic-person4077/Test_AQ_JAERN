---
title: 6 – Backfire
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single linear weapon  
==Effect:== cause backfire  
The caster casts this spell when a linear weapon is fired within range. The caster causes the gunpowder in the weapon to backfire, and the wielder of the weapon takes the damage of the attack, as if they were hit with a simple attack from the weapon. Any skills used for the attack are nullified, and the attack counts as a miss for the purposes of multiattacking. A magical weapon must make a 4d6 vs PWR to negate this effect. Linear weapons that do not rely on gunpowder are unaffected.
