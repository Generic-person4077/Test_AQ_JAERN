---
title: 13 – Defusion Field
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== aura of safety  
Defusion Field creates an aura that travels with the caster, which causes all combustible objects that enter the field to be snuffed out and unable to ignite. This will affect grenades and spells from this group, making them ineffective if they are within the aura. Objects that explode outside the radius can still capture the caster in their area of effect. A magical explosive must make a 5d6 vs PWR to negate this effect.
