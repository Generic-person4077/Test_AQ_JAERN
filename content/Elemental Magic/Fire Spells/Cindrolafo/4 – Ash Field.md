---
title: 4 – Ash Field
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 60 + 15/F’ radius  
==Effect:== field of ash  
The caster gathers energy from the elemental planes of earth and fire, combining them to produce a magical field of ash and cinders that hangs in the air, lightly obscuring the area. The area of the ash field cannot be brighter than dim lighting. A creature that moves more than 10 feet through the ash field in one round must make a 3d6 vs PER or be blinded for one round.
