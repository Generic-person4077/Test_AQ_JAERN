---
title: 7 – Lava
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 5 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== turn rock to lava  
This spell changes the indicated amount of non-magical stone into lava. Any object that touches the lava suffers 6d6 points of fire damage. A resistance check of four dice against HEA is allowed for half damage. The lava will cool at a normal rate and eventually turn back into stone.
