---
title: 2 – Lava Bullet
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single weapon  
==Effect:== turn projectile molten  
Once each round a stone or bullet fired from the affected ranged weapon is melted into magma. The projectile inflicts an additional 1d8 fire damage (bonus) on hit.
