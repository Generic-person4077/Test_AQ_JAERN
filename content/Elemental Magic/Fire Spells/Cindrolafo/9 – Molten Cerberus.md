---
title: 9 – Molten Cerberus
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== three creatures  
==Effect:== tri-headed attack  
The caster channels the power of the elemental planes of earth and fire, and fires 3 bolts of ash and lava at three creatures within range. Multiple bolts can be targeted at a single creature if desired. These bolts take the form of three canine heads which bite at the targets. Each target must make a 4d6 vs HEA to halve 3d6 fire or earth damage (caster’s choice). 

Additionally, each target is surrounded in a 10’ radius field of ash, regardless of the success of the resist check. These ash fields function identically to that of the Ash Field spell. The fields do not follow the targets.
