---
title: 8 – Magma Armor
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== grant magma armor  
Magma Armor surrounds the target with a thin, protective shield of molten earth. This grants 4/4/0 to their magic DVs. Additionally, any creature that hits the target with a combat attack takes 1d6 fire damage as the magma splashes back towards them. This damage occurs per hit.
