---
title: 16 – Ashen Phoenix
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F weeks  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== fiery death and rebirth  
When the caster dies within the duration of the spell, they explode with ash and lava. Each creature within 30 + 10/F’ of the caster must make a 6d6 vs AGI resist check to halve 8d6 of earth damage and 8d6 of fire damage, and a cloud of ash erupts in a 120 + 30/F’ radius, which functions identically to the field in the Ash Field spell. The caster is resurrected at half their Damage Points at the start of their next turn. Only one instance of Ashen Phoenix can be cast on an individual at a time.
