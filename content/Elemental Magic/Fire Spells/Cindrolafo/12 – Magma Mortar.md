---
title: 12 – Magma Mortar
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 4 + 1/F’ wide line  
==Effect:== torrent of magma  
Magma Mortar opens a rift to the intersection between the elemental planes of earth and fire, funneling magma and ash into a powerful stream. Objects and creatures in this line must make the resistance check or take 6d6 earth damage and 6d6 fire damage, halved on a success. In addition, a 30’ radius around the caster becomes an ash field, as ash spews from the rift. This ash field functions identically to that of the Ash Field spell.
