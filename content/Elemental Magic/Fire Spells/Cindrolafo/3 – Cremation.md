---
title: 3 – Cremation
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single corpse  
==Effect:== create corpse  
Cremation cremates the touched corpse and its worn belongings, leaving only a pile of ash. The spell will not work on undeads. Any worn piece of equipment must make a check of 3d6 vs its Fire Resist or be destroyed too, and magical items must make a 3d6 vs PWR check to negate this effect.
