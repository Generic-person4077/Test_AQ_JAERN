---
title: 10 – Super Chaos
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== controlled randomness  
The caster chooses a target and generates a random spell effect. They may choose up to 2 + 1/F effects within 4 + 2/F of the rolled effect if they wish. They may not choose the same effect twice and must choose at least one effect. These effects will last a maximum of 1 + 1/F days even if the duration listed is longer.
