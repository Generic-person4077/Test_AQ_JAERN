---
title: 14 – Death Lottery
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 200 + 40/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== luck of the draw  
Whichever creature in the area of effect fails their resist check by the largest amount takes 3d6 blight damage per creature in the area of effect. If there is a tie, split the damage evenly amongst all the creatures that are tied. If no one fails the resist check, nothing happens.
