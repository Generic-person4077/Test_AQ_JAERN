---
title: 9 – Chaos Master
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 200 + 40/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single chaos roll  
==Effect:== change roll  
The caster may cast this spell as a reaction to a chaos roll being made in the range. This spell causes that roll to be rerolled twice, the caster may choose any of the three rolls. The usual limit of only being able to spend EU up to a caster’s PWR per round applies, so someone with low PWR may not be able to cast this on most of their own Chaos Magics spells.
