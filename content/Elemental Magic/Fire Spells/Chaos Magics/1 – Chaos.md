---
title: 1 – Chaos
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 100 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== random  
The caster chooses a target and generates a random spell effect. This effect will last a maximum of 1 + 1/F minutes even if the duration listed is longer.
