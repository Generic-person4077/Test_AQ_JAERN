---
title: 10 – Firebreath
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== breathe flames  
Firebreath grants the caster Breath Weapon (fire) creature ability with a Potency of 8 for the duration. They may use it once a round, including the casting round, without expending a charge. If they already possess Breath Weapon (fire) their ranks of Potency are increased by 4 to a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 12 and they may use it without expending a charge.
