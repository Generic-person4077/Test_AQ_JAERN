---
title: 10 – Flame of Reversal
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== reverse healing  
If the target of this spell fails the resistance check, a phantasmal crown of fire appears above their head. For the duration, any healing effects that occur to the target deal fire/heat damage instead.
