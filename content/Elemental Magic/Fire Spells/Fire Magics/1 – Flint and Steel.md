---
title: 1 – Flint and Steel
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster, LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== self, single creature  
==Effect:== produce flame  
Flint and Steel enables the caster to snap their fingers and produce a flickering flame  in their hand. The flame can be held for 10 minutes, and sheds dim light in a 10’ radius.The caster can also throw the flame at targets, which ends the spell immediately. When this spell is cast, or as an action on a later turn, the flame can be hurled 60 + 15/F’ at a single creature, dealing 1d6 fire damage.
