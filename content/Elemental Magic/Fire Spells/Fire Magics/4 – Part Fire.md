---
title: 4 – Part Fire
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 4 + 1/F’ wide line  
==Effect:== part fire  
Part Fire allows the caster to clear a narrow path to the targeted point through continuously burning natural or magical fires (magical fires make a 3d6 vs PWR check against 9). The path must be straight. This spell is of no use against flame attacks.
