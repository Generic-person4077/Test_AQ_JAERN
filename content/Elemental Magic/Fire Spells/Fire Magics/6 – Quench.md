---
title: 6 – Quench
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== extinguish flames  
Quench snuffs out fires of the size indicated by the area of effect. Magical fires must make a 3d6 vs PWR check against 9 to negate this effect. This spell has no effect on sudden fire attacks.
