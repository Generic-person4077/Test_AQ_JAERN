---
title: 8 – Fire ‘Port
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== flaming teleport  
The caster teleports themself to an unoccupied space within the range. Immediately after they disappear, an explosion of flames erupts from the space they just left, and all creatures in a 10 + 5/F’ radius around that space must make a 4d6 vs HEA check to resist 6d6 fire damage.The caster can bring along objects and equipment they are carrying. They can also teleport one willing creature they can touch when this spell is cast. There must be space at the destination location for the creature, otherwise the creature is left behind.
