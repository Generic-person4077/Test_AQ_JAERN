---
title: 12 – The Melting
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs fire resist  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 minute  
==Area:== single object  
==Effect:== melt item  
The target is this spell is heated to extreme temperatures over the course of the casting time. If it fails a resistance check of 5d6 against its fire resistance, it melts and is destroyed. If the resistance check succeeds, it remains at the temperature for the duration but is not destroyed. The item will feel very hot, but will do no additional damage.
