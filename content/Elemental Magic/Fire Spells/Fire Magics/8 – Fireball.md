---
title: 8 – Fireball
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 40 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== explosive flames  
Fireball allows the caster to draw a bit of elemental fire from the surrounding environment and direct it to explode in a designated area. All creatures failing their resistance check take 8d6 fire damage. Dry, combustible materials can be easily set aflame with this spell.
