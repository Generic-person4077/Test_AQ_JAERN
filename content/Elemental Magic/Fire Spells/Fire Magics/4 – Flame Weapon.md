---
title: 4 – Flame Weapon
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== create flaming weapon  
This spell enables the caster to summon flames and mold it into any manual combat weapon and wield it without a strength requirement for the spell’s duration. The weapon does its base damage as fire damage and counts as magical for the purposes of bypassing protections and resistances.
