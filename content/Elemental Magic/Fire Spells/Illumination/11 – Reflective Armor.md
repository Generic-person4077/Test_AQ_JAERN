---
title: 11 – Reflective Armor
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== reflect light  
Reflective armor surrounds the caster with a mirror barrier layer that bends light and reflects all damaging light based effects back at attackers. The light can be natural or conjured and deals the same amount of damage to the original caster of the damaging spell or attacker hitting the caster with light based weaponry. Appropriate rolls to hit and resistance checks apply.
