---
title: 5 – Light Weapon
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== caster’s hand  
==Effect:== create enchanted whip  
This spell enables the caster to gather existing light into the shape of any manual weapon for the spell’s duration. This spell must be cast while the caster is in light, and dim lighting is not enough to create the weapon. The weapon deals damage appropriate for its type and is enchanted with a +1 + 1/2F weapon charm. This spell can be active multiple times, once per hand.
