---
title: 3 – Light Shield
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== bend light into shield  
Light Shield enables the caster to bend existing light in their surroundings into a circular shield, which floats in front of the caster. This spell must be cast while the caster is in light, and dim lighting is not enough to create a shield. The light is bent into a buckler (magic DVs of 1/0/0). If finessed twice, the light effectively creates an iron shield (magic DVs of 3/3/1). If fully finessed, the shield additionally subtracts 2 points from any damage the caster takes.
