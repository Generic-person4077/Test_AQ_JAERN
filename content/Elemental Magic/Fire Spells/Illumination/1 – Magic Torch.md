---
title: 1 – Magic Torch
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F objects  
==Effect:== make item glow  
Magic Torch causes an object to shed light equivalent to a normal torch: 20 feet of light, with an additional 20 feet of dim lighting.
