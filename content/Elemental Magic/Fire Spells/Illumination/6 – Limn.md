---
title: 6 – Limn
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== outline with light  
Limn is only useful when cast in a dim or dark area. It causes tendrils of light to flash from the caster’s fingertips into the described area. Each object within the area is outlined in a dim light (color of the caster’s choice). Any creature in the area must make the resist check or also be outlined in the light. For the duration, affected objects and creatures shed 5’ of dim light. This may provide easier targeting for ranged attacks and spellcasting and affected creatures cannot benefit from being Invisible.
