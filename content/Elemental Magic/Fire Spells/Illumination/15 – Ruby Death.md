---
title: 15 – Ruby Death
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs AGI halved  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== beam of deadly light  
Ruby Death enables the caster to fire a red ray of concentrated light energy from their finger. If the target fails the resistance check, it takes 15d6 points of fire/heat damage. Successful resistance check allows the target to partially dodge the beam taking half the original damage.
