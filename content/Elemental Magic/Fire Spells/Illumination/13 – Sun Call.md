---
title: 13 – Sun Call
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 60 + 15/F’ radius  
==Effect:== magical daylight  
Sun Call empowers the magician to bring the brilliance of the noon sun to the area. This counts as full lighting, as well as sunlight.
