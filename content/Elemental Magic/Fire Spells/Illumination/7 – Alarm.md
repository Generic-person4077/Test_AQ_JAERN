---
title: 7 – Alarm
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER notice*  
==Target:== LOS 100’  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== alarm entrances  
Alarm is useful to protect a room, building, campsite, etc. 2 + 1/F entrances are spanned by a faint beam of light which, when broken, emits a shrill alarm that will wake up all creatures within 100’ of the broken beam. Each beam can extend up to 20 feet, if necessary.When fully finessed, beams move through the area in the unpredictable manner, requiring 4d6 check against Acrobatics to move through it unnoticed.
