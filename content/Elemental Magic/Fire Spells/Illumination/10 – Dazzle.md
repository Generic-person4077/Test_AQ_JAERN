---
title: 10 – Dazzle
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI negates  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== Blind targets  
Dazzle causes a ball of light to appear in the caster’s hand and may be hurled up to40 + 10/F’ away. The ball explodes at the target point, producing a shower of brilliant light fragments that temporarily blind any creature in the area of effect for the spell’s duration. Creatures that don’t rely on vision automatically succeed the resistance check.
