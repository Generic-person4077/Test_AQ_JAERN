---
title: 16 – Beamrider
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== travel to light source  
Beamrider enables the caster to travel to the source of any light within their line of sight, up to 300 miles, in the blink of an eye.
