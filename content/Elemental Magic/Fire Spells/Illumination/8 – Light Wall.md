---
title: 8 – Light Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== 10’ x 10’ + 5/F’  
==Effect:== create wall of light  
Light Wall creates a 10’ x 10’ wall of light that completely blocks line-of-sight and will also absorb any light that attempts to pass through it, such as lasers or light based spells. The caster can finesse the spell to increase either dimension by 5’. Creatures, objects, and non-light energy can pass freely through the wall. The wall can be any color of the caster’s choosing.
