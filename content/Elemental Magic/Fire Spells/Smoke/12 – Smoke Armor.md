---
title: 12 – Smoke Armor
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== create magical armor  
This spell creates a suit of smoke armor that envelops the caster. It gives the defense values of (10/10/6). This armor has no movement and attribute penalties.
