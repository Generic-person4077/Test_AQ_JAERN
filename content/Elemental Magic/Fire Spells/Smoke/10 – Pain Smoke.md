---
title: 10 – Pain Smoke
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== damaging smoke  
This spell creates damaging white smoke in the area. Targets in the area of effect who breathe in the smoke will start coughing violently and take 4d6 pain damage, or half as much on a successful resist check. The resist check and damage are repeated each round a creature remains in the smoke.
