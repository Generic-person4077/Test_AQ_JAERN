---
title: 5 – Tint
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== single glass object  
==Effect:== tint glass  
Tint causes a crystal or glass object to darken and become semi-opaque. This will block half of all light from passing through the object. Light-based spells passing through the affected glass are affected accordingly (GM’s discretion). A magical item must make a 3d6 vs PWR to negate this effect.
