---
title: 16 – Killer Smoke
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10’ radius  
==Effect:== deadly smoke  
This spell summons a cloud of lethal smoke. Those in the area of effect must make the resistance check each round they remain in the area of effect or die.
