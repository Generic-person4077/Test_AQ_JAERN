---
title: 2 – Incense
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== scented smoke  
The caster creates a cloud of gray, lightly obscuring smoke that fills the area with a non-offensive odor. The exact smell of the incense is of the caster’s choosing. Visibility is reduced to 40 feet for creatures inside the cloud.
