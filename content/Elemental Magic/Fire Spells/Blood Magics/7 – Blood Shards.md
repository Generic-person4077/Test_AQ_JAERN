==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 80 + 20/F’ radius  
==Effect:== multiple blood spikes  
*Branding, Acidic 1d6*  
The caster is surrounded by 6 + 2/F floating shards of solid blood. Shards automatically hit, at caster’s discretion, anything within the spell's area of effect. A creature hit by a shard takes 1d6 physical, piercing damage. Any single creature can only be hit with a single Blood Shard per round. When fully finessed, 2 additional shards are summoned every round for the duration.
