==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs STR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== pull on blood  
*Purging*  
This spell momentarily generates a powerful attractive force centered at the targeted point. Any Branded creature within the area must succeed a resistance check of five dice vs STR or be pulled to the center of the targeted area and knocked prone. If more than one creature fails the resist check, all affected creatures take 3d6 blunt damage as they bump into each other.
