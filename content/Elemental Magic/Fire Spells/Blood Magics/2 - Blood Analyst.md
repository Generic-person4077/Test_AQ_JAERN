---
title: 2 - Blood Analyst
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 50 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== analyze blood sample  
For the duration of the spell, the caster sees blood drops in the area of effect up to 1 week old. Finesses can provide additional information about the blood:1 – Distinguish blood from different creatures2 – Race and exact number of creatures3 – Type of weapon that caused blood loss4 – In what direction creatures moved and with what speed
