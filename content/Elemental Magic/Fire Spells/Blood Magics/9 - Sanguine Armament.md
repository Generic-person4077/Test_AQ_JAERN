  - Branding

==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== blood weapons/ammo  
*Branding, Acidic 1d6*  
The caster forms part of their blood into an enchanted weapon. The weapon can be any mundane weapon type and is enchanted to +1 + 1/F. For ranged weapons, ammo can be created for 1 DP per arrow/bolt.
