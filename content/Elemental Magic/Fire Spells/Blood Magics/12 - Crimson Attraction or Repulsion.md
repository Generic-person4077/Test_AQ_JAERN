==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs STR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== trap/repel Brands  
*Purging*  
This spell creates an area of force that affects any Branded creature. The spell is used in one of two modes declared at the time of casting:Attraction: Affected creatures inside the area cannot leave the area unless they succeed at the resistance check of six dice against their STR attribute.Repulsion: Affected creatures outside of the area cannot enter it unless they succeed at the resistance check of six dice against their STR attribute.The Brand is purged at the end of the spell’s duration if the creature attempted the resistance check. If the Brand is purged by any other effect, this spell no longer affects the creature.
