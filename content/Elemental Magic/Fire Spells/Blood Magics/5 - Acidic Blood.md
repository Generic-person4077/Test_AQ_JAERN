---
title: 5 - Acidic Blood
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== acidify blood  
When the caster is damaged by a combat attack, drops of caster’s blood that leave their body become acidic and deal 4d6 acid damage to the attacker. Any attacks from the same attacker past the first deal 1d6 acid damage.
