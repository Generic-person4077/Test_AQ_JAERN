==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== recollect blood  
*Purging*  
The caster uses blood brands near them to leech off enemies’ life force. Every Branded living creature in the area takes 1d6 points of blight damage and the caster is healed for the total amount of damage dealt up to their maximum DP.
