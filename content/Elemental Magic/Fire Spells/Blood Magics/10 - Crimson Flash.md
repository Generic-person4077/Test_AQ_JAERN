==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster, LOS 120’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== teleport to Brand  
*Purging*  
For the duration of the spell, the caster may use their fifth to teleport next to any Branded creature within 120’ of themselves, which Purges the chosen creature of their Brand at the moment of teleportation.
