==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 100 + 25/F’ radius  
==Effect:== explode Brands  
*Purging*  
The caster’s blood inside Branded creatures within the area of effect explodes, dealing 10d6 fire/heat damage. When fully finessed, the caster may choose to exclude any number of Branded creatures from this effect.
