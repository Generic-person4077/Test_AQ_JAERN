==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 8 + 2/F miles  
==Effect:== track blood  
*Purging*  
For the duration of the spell, the caster can sense the distance and direction to the creature that left a blood sample or to the strongest Brand. The creature is located up to the nearest 300’. When fully finessed, the caster takes one die off any Survival: Tracking/Stalking checks to track the creature.
