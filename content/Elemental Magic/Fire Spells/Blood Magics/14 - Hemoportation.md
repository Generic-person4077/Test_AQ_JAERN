==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PWR negates*  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== self, single creature  
==Effect:== transport to or call Brand  
*Purging*  
The spell exploits the connection between the caster and the strongest active Brand. It can be used in one of two ways:Target-to-Caster: the Branded creature sinks into a pool of blood and reappears next to the caster unless they succeed a six dice resistance check against their PWR attribute.Caster-to-Target: the caster sinks into a pool of blood and reappears next to the Branded creature. The caster may not bring other creatures with them.When fully finessed this spell functions across planes.
