---
title: 15 – Poison Gas
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== create cloud of poison  
Poison Gas creates a cloud of swirling, toxic gas. All in the area of effect must make the resistance check every round they are in the gas or die. The gas can be moved by air currents but will retain its size and effect until the duration expires, at which time it simply vanishes.
