---
title: 11 – Air Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 200 + 50/F’  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== create wall of air  
Air Wall creates a wall of solid yet transparent air. The wall must be straight and can be of any size to a maximum length of 100 feet and a height of 50 feet. An attribute check of six dice against STR will allow a creature to push through. Ranged projectiles are automatically deflected off the wall, but LOS targeting of spells are unaffected.
