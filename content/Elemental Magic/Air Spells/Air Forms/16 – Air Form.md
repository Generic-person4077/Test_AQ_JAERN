---
title: 16 – Air Form
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== willing targets  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== touched creatures  
==Effect:== become wind  
The caster touches willing creatures during the casting of this spell, and upon casting, the targets and their equipment assume a gaseous form of invisible wind. The targets are Immaterial for the duration of the spell, and can control their movements and aren’t affected by other air currents. They have a flying speed of 300 feet per round, and can enter anywhere a gas can enter.
