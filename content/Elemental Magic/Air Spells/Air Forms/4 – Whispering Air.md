---
title: 4 – Whispering Air
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== hear sounds  
Whispering Air allows the caster to amplify and mute certain frequencies, sharpening the sound waves that reach their ears. They receive 2 dice off any Listen checks, or remove the extra dice on defaulting the skill if they do not have it.
