---
title: 3 – Fists of Wind
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs STR halves  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== pummel with wind  
Fists of Wind creates two solid fists of air which pummel the target. This causes the target 2d6 points of blunt damage and knocks them to the ground. If they succeed at the resistance check, they take half damage and remain standing.
