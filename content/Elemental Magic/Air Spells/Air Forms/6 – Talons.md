---
title: 6 – Talons
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== 5’ x (30 + 5/F’) cone  
==Effect:== claws of air  
This spell creates ripping claws of air which rush along in a cone from the caster’s hand. All in the cone take 4d6 edged damage and must make an attribute check of four dice against AGI. Those that fail the resistance check by 5 or more are pushed back 20’ and knocked prone. Non-living objects will also be affected by the spell, and may be pushed back or damaged as appropriate.
