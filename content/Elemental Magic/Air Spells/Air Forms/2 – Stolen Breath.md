---
title: 2 – Stolen Breath
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== knock the wind out  
The target of Stolen Breath must make the resistance check or have the air in its lungs forcibly removed, and is Stunned for the next round, as it must gasp for air. A creature that doesn’t need to breathe is immune to this effect.
