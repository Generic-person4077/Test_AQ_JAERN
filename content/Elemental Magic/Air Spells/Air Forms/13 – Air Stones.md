---
title: 13 – Air Stones
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs AGI halves  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== 5’ x (50 + 5/F’) cone  
==Effect:== bludgeoning air  
Air Stones creates hundreds of rock sized balls of solid air. These balls stream from the caster’s hand, causing 10d6 blunt damage to all in their path. Those that fail the resistance check by 5 or more are pushed back 40’ and knocked prone.
