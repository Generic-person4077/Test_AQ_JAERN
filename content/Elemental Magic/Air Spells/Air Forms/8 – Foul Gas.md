---
title: 8 – Foul Gas
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== sickening gas cloud  
This spell creates a noxious gas cloud which obscures vision and sickens those inside it. Those failing a resistance check are at -4 to all attack and skill rolls while inside. The resistance check must be made every round while inside the smoke or until failed. The gas can be moved by air currents but will retain its size and effect until the duration expires.
