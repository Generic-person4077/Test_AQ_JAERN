---
title: 1 – Sweet Breeze
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== freshen air  
Sweet breeze causes the air immediately around the target to be lightly perfumed and sweet smelling. It does not create air, nor purify the air that the target breathes.
