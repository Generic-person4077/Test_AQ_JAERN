---
title: 5 – Thunderclap
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F rounds  
==Area:== 30 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== loud thunder  
Thunderclap creates hundreds of tiny vacuums which collapse with a deafening roar. All within the area of effect must make the resistance check or be temporarily Deafened. Every 10 minutes, a Deafened creature reattempts the resistance check for their hearing to return, beginning with 3d6, and progressively at one die fewer until successful.
