---
title: 5 – Minor Illusion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 300’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== minor illusion  
This spell allows the caster to create a minor auditory, tactile, or scent illusion at the target point of the caster’s choice. This could include the sound of a bird calling or a cry for help, a tremor in the earth or a slight draft, or the smell of baked bread or burning. None of these effects will cause any harm to anyone experiencing it.
