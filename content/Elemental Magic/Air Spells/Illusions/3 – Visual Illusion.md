---
title: 3 – Visual Illusion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 300’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 10’ radius  
==Effect:== create visual illusion  
This spell allows the caster to create any static visual illusion within the constraints of the spell area. This illusion has all of the visual components of the real thing, but will not move, has no substance and produces no heat, sound, etc.
