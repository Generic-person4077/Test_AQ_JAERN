---
title: 8 – Motion Illusion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 300’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== moving visual illusion  
This spell works the same as Visual Illusion except that the illusion can move within the radius and the caster can control it. This illusion has all of the visual components of the real thing, but has no substance and produces no heat, sound, etc. With two finesses, the caster can include sound and scent into the illusion as well. With four finesses, the caster can include thermal and tactile components to the illusion (an illusory storm will look, sound, smell, and feel like a storm).
