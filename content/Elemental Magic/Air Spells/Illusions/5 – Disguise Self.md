---
title: 5 – Disguise Self
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== disguises self  
The caster first visualizes the memlocked image of a person and concentrates. Disguise Self then makes the caster appear as that person, including clothing. The caster cannot change their general size with this magic, but can adjust height by up to 1 + 1/2F feet and weight by up to 20 + 5/F lbs. Without a memlocked image the spell will change the caster’s appearance to an average member of the caster’s base species.
