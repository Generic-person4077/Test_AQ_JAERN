---
title: 2 – Blurring Aura
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== blur identity  
This spell creates a blurring aura around the target that makes them completely unrecognizable. The blur around the target is quite obvious and will clearly be out of place.
