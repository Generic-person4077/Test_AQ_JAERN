---
title: 11 – Greater Ignore
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== ignore greater illusions  
This spell will allow the target to see through any illusion magic. When this spell is active, the illusion does not at all affect them, so they are unable to see, hear, touch, etc. any illusion, unless it is their own, for the duration of the spell. This means that they are unaware that an illusion even exists.
