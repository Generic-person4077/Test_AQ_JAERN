---
title: 9 – Shroud
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== invisible shroud  
This spell will cause all creatures in the radius to be under an invisible shroud which will hide their presence to anyone, including others within the shroud. Any movement or sound by a creature within the shroud will make them visible until this movement or sound is stopped.
