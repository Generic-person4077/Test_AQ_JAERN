---
title: 9 – Phantasmal Spell
---
==Time to Cast:== special*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== special*  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== duplicate spell  
This spell allows the caster to appear to cast any elemental spell. The spell will duplicate the visual, sound and smell effects of the original spell, but will be completely harmless. It cannot duplicate any spell whose effect is visual only. It is possible for creatures to lose a round of action due to surprise.The casting time, target, duration, and area of effect are identical to the original spell, so permanent spells would last until the phantasmal spell is dropped or revoked and the spells can be plugged as normal via the Maintained Cast (CS).
