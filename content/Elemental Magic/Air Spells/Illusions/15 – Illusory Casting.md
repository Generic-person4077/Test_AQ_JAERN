---
title: 15 – Illusory Casting
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== make spells illusions  
If the target of this spell fails the resistance check, all of the spells (with the exception of Revocation) cast by them for the duration are cast as illusions. They will have all the visual, sound, and smell components, but will not have any other effect and cause no damage. The caster of these spells must still spend units for the cast spells.
