---
title: 16 – Living Nightmare
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== deepest fears  
Drawing on the deepest fears of a group of creatures, the caster creates nightmarish illusions in their minds, visible only to them. Each creature in the area must make a 6d6 vs WIL check. On a fail, a creature becomes Frightened for the duration. The illusion calls on the creature's deepest fears, manifesting its worst nightmares as an implacable threat. At the end of each of a Frightened creature's turns, it must reattempt the 6d6 vs WIL resist check, or take 4d10 mental damage. On a successful save, there is no damage and the spell ends for that creature.
