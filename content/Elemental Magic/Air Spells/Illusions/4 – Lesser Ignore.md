---
title: 4 – Lesser Ignore
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== see through illusions  
This spell will allow the caster to see through any illusion of spell rank 7 or lower (or an equivalent form of magic). When this spell is active, the illusion is completely ignored, so they are unable to see, hear, touch, etc. any illusion, unless it is their own, for the duration of the spell. This means that they are unaware that such an illusion even exists.
