---
title: 14 – Displacement
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== displace self  
This spell makes the caster invisible for the duration, and also creates an illusionary version of them 10 + 2/F’ away from the caster. This illusion is semi-intelligent and will move as if an independent entity behaving in a similar fashion as the caster is acting. All sound, smell, and heat given off by the caster instead comes from the illusion, but the illusion is not solid and is not affected by targeted attacks or spells (except Revoke) and cannot take any damage.
