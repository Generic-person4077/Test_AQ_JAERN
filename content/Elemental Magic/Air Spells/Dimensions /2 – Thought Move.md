---
title: 2 – Thought Move
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single object  
==Effect:== move object  
Thought Move allows movement of a non-living, free standing object of 20 + 5/F pounds or less that is in the caster’s LOS, from one place to another within the target range. Since the motion is slow, no damage is inflicted from striking other objects, unless circumstances dictate otherwise (walking into a set spear, falling from a height, etc.).
