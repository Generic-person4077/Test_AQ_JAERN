---
title: 4 – Peer
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== memlocked location  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 60’ radius  
==Effect:== see through rift  
Peer enables the caster to monitor any memlocked location via stable pocket Rift for the spell’s duration. Only sight is allowed; no sounds, smells, etc. can be discovered. The Rift opening on the other side will appear to be a wavering indistinct space in the air. There is a 4d6 vs PER check to notice the wavering air.
