---
title: 7 – Spatial Fold
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ cube  
==Effect:== private battle  
This spell folds a 20 + 5/F’ cube of space onto itself, centered on the caster, trapping any creatures and objects within the cube with the caster in the fold. From the inside, the edges of the space appear waving, like a mirage. From the outside, a two-dimensional shimmering fold that occupies a 5’ cube marks where the space used to be. Creatures and objects can enter or exit the fold by making a 4d6 vs PWR check.
