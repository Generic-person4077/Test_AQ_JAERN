---
title: 15 – Otherworld
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 5’  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== open portal  
The caster must cast this on still air in the area of effect. When the spell is cast, the caster concentrates on a dimension and the place within that dimension where the portal will lead, but doesn’t need to have this location memlocked. A visible portal opens, providing a corridor to the target location. Anyone can then step through this portal, and be there. The corridor cannot be opened onto the same dimension from which it is cast. With four finesses, this portal can be bound to an object (such as a key) allowing the portal to be moved. The destination will remain the same.
