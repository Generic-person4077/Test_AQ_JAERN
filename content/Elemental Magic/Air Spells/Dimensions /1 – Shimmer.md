---
title: 1 – Shimmer
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== increase combat DV  
While under the influence of Shimmer, the target’s physical form appears indistinct and wavering, effectively increasing their combat magic defense value by 2 + 1/F. The caster must be touched by air while casting the spell.
