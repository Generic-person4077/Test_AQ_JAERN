---
title: 14 – Vanish
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== send to pocket dimension  
The caster and the target must be touching air for this spell to work. If the target fails the resistance check, they are immediately dispatched to a small, life-supporting pocket dimension for the duration of the spell (the pocket dimension is  populated with monotone fruit-bearing trees and shrubs and has a spring running through it).
