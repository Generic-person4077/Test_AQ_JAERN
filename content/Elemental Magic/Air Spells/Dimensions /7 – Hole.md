---
title: 7 – Hole
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 6 + 6/F” radius  
==Effect:== create hole  
This creates a circular hole that is 6 + 6/F inches deep in any non-living object. No light can pass through this hole, making it appear totally black, but objects and creatures can pass through with no resistance. Magical objects that succeed at a resistance check of 4d6 against PWR will not pass through the hole. If the hole pierces through to the other side of the object, it also appears completely black on that side.
