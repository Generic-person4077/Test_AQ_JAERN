---
title: 9 – Abjurative Rift
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single attack  
==Effect:== redirect attack  
The caster creates a small tear in the fabric of reality that protects them from an incoming combat or ranged attack they can see. A Rift is opened up in the attack’s way which redirects it through a second Rift that the caster opens up next to a creature within the range. The attack passes through one rift and out the other, and it is treated as targeting that creature’s DVs instead. This spell can be used to cause an attack to hit the attacker themself if desired.
