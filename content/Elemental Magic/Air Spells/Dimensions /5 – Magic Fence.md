---
title: 5 – Magic Fence
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 40 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== exclude summoning  
When this spell is cast, the area centered on the caster is bounded by a glowing boundary (shaped to the caster’s choosing). Other planer and immaterial creatures, and magically transported objects cannot pass this line unless the resistance check is made (objects save against the WIL of the caster of the spell transporting it).
