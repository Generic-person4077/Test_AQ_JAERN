---
title: 16 – Dimensional Well
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== trap in Planar Sea  
Using this spell, the caster creates a jagged rift to the Planar Sea (see sidebar below) on the ground, which begins sucking in any loose objects in the radius. Any creatures standing in the area when the spell is cast must make a 4d6 vs PWR to avoid being pulled into the portal. Any creature that ends its turn in the area must attempt the check or be pulled in, and this check is repeated each round. After the spell ends the portal closes, not to be opened again.
