---
title: 10 – Pocket
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== multitouch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== touched targets  
==Effect:== open elemental pocket  
When this spell is cast, a small opening to a small elemental demiplane appears suspended in air, visible only to the caster and anyone they touch during the casting time. If they enter, they appear to vanish into thin air. Normal humanoid life is supported in the dimension, and at the end of the duration anyone still within the dimension is pushed out to their starting point. The caster must be touching air for this magic to work.
