---
title: 11 – Portable Hole
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== 6 + 6/F” radius  
==Effect:== create portable hole  
This spell creates a dimensional hole in any non-living object identical to the Hole spell. However, grasping the hole by the outside edge, the Portable Hole can be peeled off, rolled up, and reused elsewhere. Magical objects get a resistance check of 4d6 against PWR to negate the effect.
