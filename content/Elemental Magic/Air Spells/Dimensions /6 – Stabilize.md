---
title: 6 – Stabilize
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 5’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== single unstable RIft  
==Effect:== stabilize portals  
Stabilize will allow the caster to stabilize unstable portals extending their duration by 4 + 2/F hours, after which they will close. This spell cannot be cast on the same portal twice. This spell can extend the duration of all portals and Rifts created by this group.
