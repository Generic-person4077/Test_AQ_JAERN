---
title: 3 – Arise
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== rise from ground  
Arise creates a compressed pocket of air beneath the caster, allowing them to float into the air. The caster, while concentrating with their action, can float up or down at a rate of 4 + 1/F’ per round. The caster is at the mercy of winds and other forces that could cause lateral movement. They cannot carry more than they could normally lift above their head.
