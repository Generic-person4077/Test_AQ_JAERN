---
title: 8 – Interference
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 60 + 15/F’ radius  
==Effect:== disrupt electromagnetism  
For the duration of the spell, the caster produces a large quantity of electromagnetic noise. Any spells that utilize electricity or lightning require a 4d6 vs PWR check to cast. Additionally, forms of communication and items reliant on electricity are non-functional in the area unless they make the resistance check.
