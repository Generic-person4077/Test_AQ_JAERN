---
title: 3 – Modify Resistivity
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== electrical susceptibility  
If the target fails the resistance check, they gain one rank of Susceptible to Lightning for the duration of the spell.
