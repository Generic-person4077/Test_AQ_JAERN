---
title: 6 – Lightning Bolt
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 4 + 1/F’ wide line  
==Effect:== bolt of electricity  
The caster uses this spell to create opposed electrical charges between the caster’s fingertip and their intended target. This electrical potential causes a bolt of electrical energy to travel from their finger to the target along the path of least resistance. Intervening objects and people along this path are affected by the bolt, as is the target, taking 6d6 lightning damage.
