---
title: 15 – Elektrafulmo
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 200 + 50/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== blinding lightning  
The target of this spell gets struck by a blinding bolt from the sky, taking 14d6 lightning damage, or half on a successful resistance check. All those within 50 feet of the target half of that damage (quarter damage if they make the resistance check). Creatures within 100’ of the target must make a 4d6 vs HEA or go blind until healed (creatures that do not rely on eyesight are not affected by this).
