---
title: 13 – Vacuum
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== remove air  
Vacuum creates an airless space in the area of effect. Creatures that require air who fail the resistance check fall Unconscious. Fires are immediately extinguished, any winds becalmed, and other processes involving air are likewise halted. The GM will adjudicate any side-effects of the sudden decompression.
