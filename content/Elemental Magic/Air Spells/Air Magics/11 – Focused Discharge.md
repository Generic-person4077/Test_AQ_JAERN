---
title: 11 – Focused Discharge
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== electrical explosion  
A large bolt of lightning shoots down from the sky, hitting the radius, and everything above it. Creatures in the area of effect take 10d6 points of lightning damage.
