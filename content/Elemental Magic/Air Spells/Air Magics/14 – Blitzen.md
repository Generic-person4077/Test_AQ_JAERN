---
title: 14 – Blitzen
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 8 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== lightning storm  
Blitzen causes a storm of lightning to gather overhead, and calls lightning to strike a point within 60 + 15/F’ radius of the caster. All creatures in a 10’ radius of the lightning strike suffer 6d6 lightning damage. The caster, as a fifth, can cause another bolt of lightning to strike on subsequent turns during the duration of the spell. If this spell is cast during a thunderstorm, the damage is doubled to 12d6 per strike.
