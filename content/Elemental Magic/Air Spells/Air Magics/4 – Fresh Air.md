---
title: 4 – Fresh Air
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== freshen air  
With Fresh Air the caster can cleanse a volume of fetid, putrid, or poisonous air to  make it breathable. Active spell effects are not negated by this effect. The purified area can move with the caster if they so choose.
