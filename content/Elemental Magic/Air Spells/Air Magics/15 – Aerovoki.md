---
title: 15 – Aerovoki
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== summon air elementals  
Aerovoki summons 4 + 2/F air elementals to any point within the range and the caster’s LOS. Air must be available, of course.Refer to Appendix D: Spell Clarification for creature write ups and control mechanics.
