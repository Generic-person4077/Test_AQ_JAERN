---
title: 4 – Clear!
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch*  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 + 1/2F targets  
==Effect:== shock to life  
When used on a target, this spell allows the caster to take one die off a skill check against the Medicine: Revive skill. For every 2 finesses, it allows the caster to simultaneously roll a check to revive one other person, within 5 feet. If the caster has no skill, this will act as a successful one die check against the Medicine: Revive skill.
