---
title: 6 – Chain Lightning
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== arcing electricity  
The caster channels energy from the elemental plane of air to fire a bolt of lightning at a target, which then arcs to 3 + 1/2F other targets within 30 + 10/F’ of the first target dealing 6d6 points of lightning damage to all affected creatures.
