---
title: 9 – Overcharge
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single lightning spell  
==Effect:== high voltage  
This spell is cast as a reaction when the caster casts a spell that deals lightning damage. Any lightning or electric-based spell cast in conjunction with this spell ignores 2 + 1/2F ranks of Resist Lightning. Additionally, True Immunity Lightning will only halve damage instead of granting full immunity to the caster’s lightning spell damage.
