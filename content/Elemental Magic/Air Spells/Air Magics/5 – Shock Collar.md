---
title: 5 – Shock Collar
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single target  
==Effect:== punish action  
While casting this spell, the mage specifies an action of three words or less, such as “casting spells” or “hurting innocent people” and precedes it with the word “No.” Though there is no check to be able to disobey the command, they take 2d6 points of lightning damage every time they go against it. This activates (and therefore deals damage) at most, once per round.Specified actions can only include things that people can do voluntarily; actions like “no breathing” will not function with this spell unless breathing is optional for a target.
