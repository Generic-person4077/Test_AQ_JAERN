---
title: 14 – Sensitivity to EM
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F days  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== read EM signals  
This spell gives the caster the ability to perceive and make sense of electromagnetic waves. This would allow for them to intercept radio, and understand the flow of electricity through an object, among other things.
