---
title: 10 – Anaerobia
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== survive without air  
This spell satisfies the target’s need for oxygen, making normal breathing unnecessary. Anaerobia does not protect the target from possible physical damage caused by hostile environments, such as fire, extreme cold, etc.
