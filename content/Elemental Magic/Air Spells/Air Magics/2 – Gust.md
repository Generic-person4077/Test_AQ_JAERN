---
title: 2 – Gust
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 10’ x (20 + 10/F’) line  
==Effect:== strong gust of wind  
Gust produces a blast of air in the area of effect, up to 30 + 10/F miles per hour, sufficient to extinguish unprotected flames, fan a large fire, propel a sailed vessel, etc. The caster can use an action on subsequent turns during the spell’s duration to control the speed and direction of the gust.
