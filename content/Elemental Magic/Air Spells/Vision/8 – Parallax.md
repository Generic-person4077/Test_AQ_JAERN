---
title: 8 – Parallax
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== blur body  
Parallax causes the light around the target’s body to shift about suddenly. Attackers must try to correct for the sudden parallax error, in effect raising all the target’s magic Defense Values by 2 + 1/F.
