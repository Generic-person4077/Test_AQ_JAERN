---
title: 4 – Lens
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== bend LOS  
Lens is cast when the caster casts another line-of-sight ranged spell, targeting a creature that is behind cover. Lens adjusts the density of air between the caster and the target, bending line of sight for the caster so that they can target the coupled spell around cover.
