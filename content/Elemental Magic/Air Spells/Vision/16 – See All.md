---
title: 16 – See All
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== create LOS  
See All enables the caster to see through solid objects and creatures. Thus, the caster can cast a line of sight spell through any interposing creature or object subject to that spell’s normal range restrictions.
