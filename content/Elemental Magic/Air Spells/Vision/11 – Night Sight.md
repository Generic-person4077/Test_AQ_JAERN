---
title: 11 – Night Sight
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== see in the dark  
Night Sight enables the target to see in natural darkness and shadows as well as he can see in bright moonlight. This grants the target 4 + 1/F ranks of Night Vision. If the effect is fully finessed, they can even see in magical darkness or shadows.
