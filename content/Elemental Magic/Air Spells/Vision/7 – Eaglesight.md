---
title: 7 – Eaglesight
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== magnify vision  
Eaglesight enables the caster to see minute details that escape normal vision by magnifying things within their vision. For the duration, the caster gains 1 + 1/2F ranks of Exceptional PER.
