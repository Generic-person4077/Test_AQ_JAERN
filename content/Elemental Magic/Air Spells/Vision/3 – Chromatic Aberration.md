---
title: 3 – Chromatic Aberration
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== bend colors  
The caster causes light to scatter and bend off of the target, creating multicolored afterimages to the left and right of them. This slightly obscures their movements, and grants a 1 + 1/2F bonus to movement Defense Values.
