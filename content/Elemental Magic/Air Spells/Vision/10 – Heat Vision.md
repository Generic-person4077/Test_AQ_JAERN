---
title: 10 – Heat Vision
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== see heat  
The recipient of this spell is empowered to see heat in addition to light. For the duration, the target gains 4 + 2/F ranks of Heat Vision.
