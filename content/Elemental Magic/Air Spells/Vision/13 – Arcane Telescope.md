---
title: 13 – Arcane Telescope
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single LOS spell  
==Effect:== extend targeting  
Arcane Telescope is cast as a reaction when the caster casts a LOS spell. This extends the targeting distance limit of a LOS spell up to 2,000,000 feet.
