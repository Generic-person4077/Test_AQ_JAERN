---
title: 14 – Mass Blind
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== 3 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== removes vision  
Mass Blind makes the targets Blinded for all forms of vision, both physical and magical for the duration. Echolocation and Tremor Sense do not count as vision for this spell.
