---
title: 11 – Sight License
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== bestows sight  
The recipient of a Sight License is granted the power of sight, even if it is blind or lacks sight organs.
