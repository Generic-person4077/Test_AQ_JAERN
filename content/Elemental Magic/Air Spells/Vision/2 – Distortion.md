---
title: 2 – Distortion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PER negates*  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F’ minutes  
==Area:== 5 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== obscure vision  
Distortion causes light rays to bend and shift erratically in the area of effect. Forms within the area become blurry, indistinct, and require outside observers to make a 4d6 vs PER check to make out. If failed, this spell imposes a -4 penalty to ranged attacks against targets in the area, and makes spell targeting impossible.
