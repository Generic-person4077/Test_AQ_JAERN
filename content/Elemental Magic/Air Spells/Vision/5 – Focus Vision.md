---
title: 5 – Focus Vision
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== increase perception  
Focus Vision allows the caster to temporarily increase the target’s PER by 2 + 1/F for the duration of the spell.
