---
title: 15 – Magic Vision
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== magic sight  
Magic Vision allows the target to see magical items and effects. This grants the target 4 + 2/F ranks of See Magic for the duration. Additionally, the target can see auras of magic around creatures, with varying orders of brightness that correspond to their units and magical creature abilities. The target automatically knows the type and power of the magic, without having to make Arcana checks to identify.
