---
title: 11 – Cataract
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== cloud vision  
If the target fails the resistance check, then they develop cataracts in both of their eyes. The lenses of their eyes cloud over and their vision is reduced to 120 - 30/F feet.
