---
title: 14 – Burning Smoke
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs HEA halves  
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 40 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== acidic smoke  
The caster summons a small fire which creates a cloud of blue, acidic smoke. The acid suspended in the smoke will cause 5d6 acid damage to any creature in the area of effect. The resist check and damage are repeated each round a creature remains in the smoke. The cloud can be moved by natural or magical wind, but will retain its shape for the duration of the spell. Fresh Air or similar spells will not purify the area.
