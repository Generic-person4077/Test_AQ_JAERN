---
title: 9 – Spirit Smoke
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== special*  
==Target:== LOS 40 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== spirit revealing smoke  
This spell creates a light red smoke that fills the area and outlines all ethereal creatures and spirits. If the creature fails a 4d6 vs PWR check, they become material, obtaining a form that best fits their shape, and maintaining the ability to fly. They will become immaterial again one round after they exit the smoke.
