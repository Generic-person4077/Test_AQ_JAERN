---
title: 3 – Smog
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs HEA negates  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== unpleasant smoke  
The caster summons a small fire, which creates a brown, lightly obscuring, noxious cloud of smoke that contains dust and other fine particles. Anyone in the area of effect that fails the resistance check begins coughing and is Dazed. They reattempt the resist check at the beginning of each turn while within the smog. Visibility is reduced to 40 feet for creatures inside the cloud.
