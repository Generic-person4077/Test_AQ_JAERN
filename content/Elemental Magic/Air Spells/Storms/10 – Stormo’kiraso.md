---
title: 10 – Stormo’kiraso
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== storm armor  
The target is granted armor of dark billowing clouds, which flashes with internal lightning. While wearing the armor, the target gains a rank of Resist Lightning and Resist Water. Additionally, any creature that hits the target with a combat attack takes 1d6 lightning damage as it arcs from the armor. This damage occurs per hit.
