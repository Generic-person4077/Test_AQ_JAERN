---
title: 2 – Forecast
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== noen  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== know weather  
The caster beseeches the elemental spirits of the wind around them, and learns the weather forecast for the next 4 + 1/F days. This would be the natural weather that will occur, barring any magic or unpredictable effects.
