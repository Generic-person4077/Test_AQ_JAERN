---
title: 1 – Umbrella
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 30 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== block rain  
Umbrella creates a force field a foot above the target’s head that follows them. This field will protect them from drizzle or light rain. The water will slide off the field and not touch the caster. This will prevent the Drenched condition from light rain.
