---
title: 13 – Planar Contact
---
==Time to Cast:== 10 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== 3 + 1/2F questions  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== contact extraplanar mind  
The caster mentally contacts a servitor, spirit, or other mysterious entity from another plane. If they have such a target memlocked, they can directly contact that specific target, otherwise, the contact is determined by the GM (they may ask for a high-or-low roll for a benevolent or malevolent contact). The caster can then ask 3 + 1/2F questions, which the extraplanar contact answers.
