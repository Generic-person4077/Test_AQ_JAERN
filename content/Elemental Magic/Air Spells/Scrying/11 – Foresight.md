---
title: 11 – Foresight
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 3 + 1/F questions  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== see into future  
Foresight enables the caster to determine what will happen in the immediate future (1 + 1/F days) by asking yes-or-no questions to the elemental spirits around them. The truthfulness of the answers is from 60 + 5/F percent. If not truthful, responses can be totally false, or couched as half-truths (GM’s discretion).
