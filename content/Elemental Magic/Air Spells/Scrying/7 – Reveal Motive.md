---
title: 7 – Reveal Motive
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 2 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== see motive  
The caster targets 2 + 1/F creatures within range and instantly learns their surface motivations, and they must make a 4d6 vs WIL resist check or the caster knows context and true motivations, regardless of any surface deceptions. If any magical effects exist to deceive this spell, roll a contested 7d6 against the other spell’s rank in d6 to pierce through the deception.
