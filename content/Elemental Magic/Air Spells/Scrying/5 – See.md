---
title: 5 – See
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== remote vision  
The caster selects an area within range, but not necessarily in LOS, then casts See. They may then see everything in the area as if their eyes were there. After casting See, the caster must remain motionless and concentrate or the spell is broken. When fully finessed, the caster can alternatively target a location they have memlocked for this spell.
