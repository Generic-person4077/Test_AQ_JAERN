---
title: 12 – Reveal Legends
---
==Time to Cast:== 10 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== special*  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== special*  
==Effect:== know lore  
Reveal Legends is used to gain knowledge of the items, places, and people of antiquity. The caster can learn from 3 + 1/2F pieces of information about the subject in the form of clues, or as a range of numbers. The information is always truthful, but the player must make correct interpretations. This could include previous owners of an item, or notable deeds performed by a person, or battles that occurred at a site. Any Lore checks about the subject are two dice easier for the next hour.
