---
title: 2 – Hear
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== 120 + 30/F’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 30 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== remote hearing  
The caster first chooses an area within range, which does not have to be in their LOS. They may then hear, but not necessarily understand, any noises or speech in the area as if their ears were there. After casting Hear, the caster must remain motionless and concentrate or the spell is broken. When fully finessed, the caster can alternatively target a location they have memlocked for this spell.
