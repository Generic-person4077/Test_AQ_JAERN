---
title: 14 – Miskarburo
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== backfire scrying  
Miskarburo protects the caster from any scrying attempts, and also reverses the process so that they find out what information the opposing scryer was seeking from them, imposing a 6d6 vs PWR check to negate. The caster also learns the instantaneous direction and distance of the attempt.
