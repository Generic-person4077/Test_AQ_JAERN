---
title: 16 – Mind Scan
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 200 + 50/F’ radius  
==Effect:== read minds  
The caster can read surface level thoughts of any creature within the area of effect. Any scrying spell casted while this effect is active has its resistance check increased to 6 dice.By reading minds of surrounding creatures, the caster may reduce all resistance or attribute checks imposed as a result of creatures’ actions by 2 dice. Inanimate hazards such as traps or environmental hazards are unaffected.
