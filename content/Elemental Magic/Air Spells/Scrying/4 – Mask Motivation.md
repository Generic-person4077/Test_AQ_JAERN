---
title: 4 – Mask Motivation
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== 1 + 1/F creatures  
==Effect:== mask motivation  
Mask Motivation enables the caster to obscure that information, or feed false information (of the caster’s choice), to anyone using spells or other magic to detect or reveal it.
