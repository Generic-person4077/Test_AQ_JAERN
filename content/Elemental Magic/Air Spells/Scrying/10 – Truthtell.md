---
title: 10 – Truthtell
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 5d6 vs WIL partial*  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== compel candor  
Any creature failing the resistance check must answer all the caster’s questions truthfully and completely for the duration of the spell. If they succeed the resist check, they cannot speak a deliberate lie, but can either stay silent or skirt around the truth.
