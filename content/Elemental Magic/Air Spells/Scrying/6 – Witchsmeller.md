---
title: 6 – Witchsmeller
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== detect magic  
Any enchanted items in the area of effect glow with a silver aura in the eyes of the caster. Very magical items, such as relics or artifacts, glow with a gold aura. Other spells or skills must be used to gain more knowledge (e.g. Know Element, etc.).
