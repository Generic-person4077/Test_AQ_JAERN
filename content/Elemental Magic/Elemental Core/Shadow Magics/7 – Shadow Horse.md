---
title: 7 – Shadow Horse
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== caster  
==Effect:== create equine  
Shadow Horse calls forth an equine creature from any natural shadow. This can be a horse to be ridden, a mule for carrying heavy items, etc. The creature functions in all respects like its normal counterpart, except it does not need food or water.
