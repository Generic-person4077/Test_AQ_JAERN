---
title: 6 – Shadow Hide
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== caster’s shadow  
==Effect:== make a servant  
Shadow Hide is an enhancement of Shadow Carry. Items given to the shadow are not visible while being carried.
