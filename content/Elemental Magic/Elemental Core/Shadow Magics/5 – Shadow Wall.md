---
title: 5 – Shadow Wall
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 5’  
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds  
==Area:== 10’ x 10’ x 3”  
==Effect:== solid wall  
The caster creates a solid wall of shadow that hinders light passing through it, however non-light based projectiles and people can freely pass through it. Any light-based projectiles or light-based energy get a -3 to damage. Targeting of LOS spells through the wall is hindered and requires a successful 2 dice check against Arcana.
