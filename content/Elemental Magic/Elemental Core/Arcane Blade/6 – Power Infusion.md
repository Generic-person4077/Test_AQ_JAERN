---
title: 6 – Power Infusion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single weapon  
==Effect:== empower  
The caster infuses their weapon with part of their power. For the duration, their PWR is lowered by 1 + 1/4F, but the bonus to hit and damage (bonus) on the weapon attack is increased by the same amount, stacking with Weapon Charm or permanent enchantments past their normal limits.
