---
title: 15 – Power Exchange
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== convert PWR  
The caster gains the permanent ability to spend a round to convert some of their power into STR, HEA, or AGI. Caster adds up to 8 to the chosen attribute, but loses the same amount of PWR. Only one attribute can be altered at the same time.. This effect cannot be used to raise an attribute above 20.
