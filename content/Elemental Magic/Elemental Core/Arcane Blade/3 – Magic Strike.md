---
title: 3 – Magic Strike
---
==Time to Cast:== none  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== add magic damage  
The caster uses this spell in combination with an attempt to strike an opponent. This attack adds 1d6 + 2/F points of magical damage (skill).
