---
title: 12 – Spell Storage
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours  
==Area:== single spell  
==Effect:== store spell  
The caster prepares a piece of Papyrus with their power, then has another caster channel knowledge of an elemental spell into the scroll (rank up to the original caster’s PWR/2), allowing the user of the scroll to cast the spell out of their own unit pool for the duration of the scroll.
