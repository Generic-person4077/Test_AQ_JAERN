---
title: 6 – Scroll Copying
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single scroll  
==Effect:== copy scroll  
The caster may use this spell to copy another magical scroll from this group they have completed, to give them another copy of that scroll. Copies may not be copied and any scroll may only be copied once.
