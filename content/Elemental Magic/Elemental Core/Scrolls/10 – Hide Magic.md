---
title: 10 – Hide Magic
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== hide magical output  
The caster creates a scroll that, when activated, absorbs all magical auras on the object or creature; the scroll itself also does not glow under magical detections, as it just feeds on the residual energy. A 3-die Arcana: Identify Elemental/Divine, in conjunction with the capacity to detect magic can be used to negate this.
