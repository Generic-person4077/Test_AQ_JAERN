---
title: 16 – Mindspeak
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 minutes  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== memlocked creature  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== voiceless speech  
Mindspeak allows the caster to open two-way mental communications with anyone they have memorized, regardless of where they are, for the duration of the spell.
