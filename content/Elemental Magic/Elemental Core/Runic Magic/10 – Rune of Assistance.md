---
title: 10 – Rune of Assistance
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one creature  
==Effect:== adjust roll  
Body rune. Upon failing resistance or skill check, the user can activate this rune and modify the roll by 2. The rune then disappears.
