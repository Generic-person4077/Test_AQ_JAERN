---
title: 11 – Armor Charm
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== one piece of armor  
==Effect:== enchant armor  
Armor Charm causes any armor to function as if once enchanted for the duration of the spell. Using armor thus enchanted gives its wearer 1 + 1/2F magic DVs. Multiple castings of this spell on a single target will stack with each other and permanent enchantments, up to +5.
