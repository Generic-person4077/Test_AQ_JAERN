---
title: 7 – Find
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 300 + 150/F mets  
==Effect:== locate material  
The caster concentrates on a particular material and casts this spell. If the material is within the area of the spell, an image of the material will appear in the general direction of its location. This spell locates the material to the nearest met and is fairly useless at close range. The caster cannot use this spell to locate a particular object.
