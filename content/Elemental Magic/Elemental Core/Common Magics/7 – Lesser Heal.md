---
title: 7 – Lesser Heal
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 10 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== heals all in area  
All within the area that are willing or fail the resistance check are healed 1d6 DP.
