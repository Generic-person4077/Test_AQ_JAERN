---
title: 15 – Know Magic
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 1 magical object  
==Effect:== know 2 + 1/F spells  
Know Magic enables the mage to discover which spells were used to create a magical item. Up to six spells from the caster’s background can be discovered. Spells outside the caster’s background will not be revealed unless the mage first casts Know Element to discover their presence.
