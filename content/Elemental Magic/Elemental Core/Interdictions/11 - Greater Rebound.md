---
title: 11 - Greater Rebound
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster, 90 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== redirect spell  
Activate this spell upon a spell specifically targeting you within the range. Roll 10d6 Counterspell, rebound on success.
