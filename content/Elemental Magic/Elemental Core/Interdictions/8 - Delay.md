---
title: 8 - Delay
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== touch, LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== block minor spell  
The target activates this spell upon a spell cast from within the range. If the spell is of rank 6 or below, its casting time is increased by 1 round.
