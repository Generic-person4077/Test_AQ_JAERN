This ritual takes 1 hour to complete and can be performed by any priest of Vormaxia with at least 36 total ranks in Vormaxia spells, or any Psion with a rank 12 Telepathy ability and a rank 10 ability in two other disciplines.  

Attunement is one of the highest levels of magic available. It reshapes the target’s mind, allowing for easier learning of new skills, spells and abilities. The gold cost of this ritual varies depending on what the target wants to attune for.  


| Cost (Gold) | Attune Type                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1500        | Non-combat specializations, nomadic incants |
| 2000        | Combat skills, Mods, Rogue Creeds           |
| 2500        | Elemental and Divine spell groups           |
| 3000        | Psionic discipline                          |

The ritual removes any base experience cost multipliers from the attuned ability. Any experience spent on acquiring the attuned ability is refunded, but must be first spent on buying up ability to the same level as prior to the attunement.  

This ritual is very taxing on the target’s mind. A creature may not have more abilities attuned than their non-modified INT attribute.
