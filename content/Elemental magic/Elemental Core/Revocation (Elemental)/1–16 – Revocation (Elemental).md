---
title: 1–16 – Revocation (Elemental)
---
==Time to Cast:== 3 - 1/2F rounds*
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 80 + 20/F’
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== 1 magical effect
==Effect:== nullify elemental power
The Revocation spell disrupts other magical effects. Roll (rank of Revocation)d6 Counterspell targeting one magical effect within range.When targeting enchanted items, roll Counterspell as normal. On success, the item turns into a mundane version of itself for 1 hour per rank of Revocation.The Elemental Revocation spell may be used on divine, psionic, or other non-elemental effects and items but receives a 2-1/2F die penalty on the Counterspell roll.
