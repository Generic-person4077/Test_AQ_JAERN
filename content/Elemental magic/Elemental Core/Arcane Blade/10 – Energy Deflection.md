---
title: 10 – Energy Deflection
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 reaction*
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== self
==Effect:== deflect energy
This spell can be used as a reaction to the caster being hit with magical damage while their weapon is drawn. The incoming energy is redirected into the weapon and is released with the caster's next attack, adding half of the absorbed damage to the attack (if multiple enemies are hit, divide damage equally between them) as pure magical damage. Only one source of damage can be absorbed. If energy is not used within the next round, it dissipates harmlessly. The use of this spell puts a huge toll on the caster and they lose one point of PWR for every use of this ability. Lost points are recovered at the rate of 1 point per day of rest.
