---
title: 16 – Phoenix’s Spirit
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 day
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== special*
==Area:== self
==Effect:== restore body
Phoenix’s Spirit allows the caster’s body to be fully restored in the event of death. One minute after the caster dies, their body is wreathed in green, harmless flames. Over the next five minutes, the caster’s body is fully healed and brought back to life. The caster is unconscious for the next hour. This spell lasts indefinitely until used. Only one Phoenix’s Spirit can be active at any given time.
