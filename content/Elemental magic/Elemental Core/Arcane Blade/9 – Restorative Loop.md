---
title: 9 – Restorative Loop
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds
==Area:== self
==Effect:== combat restoration
The caster creates an energy loop between themself and the weapon in their hand, rapidly moving energy between the two and utilizing it to their benefit. For the duration, the user heals DP equal to the main Weapon Charm or similar enchantment of the weapon every round (holding a +3 sword restores 3 DP per round). The enchantment must be part of the weapon and not the user’s body, thus this effect will not apply to Ergokinetic Claws or similar effects.
