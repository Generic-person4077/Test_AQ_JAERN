---
title: 1 – Convert Power
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== self
==Effect:== convert power
This spell allows the caster to convert some of their PWR into physical ability. For the duration of the spell, the caster adds 1 + 1/F to their STR, AGI, or HEA (chosen when the spell is cast) while losing the same amount of PWR. Only a single instance of this spell can be in effect on a target regardless of the attribute chosen. This effect cannot be used to raise attributes above 18.
