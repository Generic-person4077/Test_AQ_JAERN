---
title: 12 – Single Strike of Hell
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== self
==Effect:== force critical hit
The caster uses this spell in combination with an attempt to strike an opponent. The caster makes a simple attack against the enemy. If they hit, it is considered a critical hit. If they miss, they suffer a critical hit with base 1d12 of damage (damage type depends on caster’s weapon). For every 2 finesses, the caster adds +1 to critical hit roll, however the same bonus will be applied if the caster misses.
