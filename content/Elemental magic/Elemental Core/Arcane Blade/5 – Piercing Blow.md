---
title: 5 – Piercing Blow
---
==Time to Cast:== none
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== self
==Effect:== ignore armor
The caster uses this spell in combination with an attempt to strike an opponent. The attack is made against touch DVs of the target. This spell cannot be used in combination with other spells that require a hit to activate, such as Foolhardy.
