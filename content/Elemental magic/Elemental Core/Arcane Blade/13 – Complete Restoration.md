---
title: 13 – Complete Restoration
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== self
==Effect:== repair body
Once cast, this spell completely restores the caster's body. Damage points are fully restored, any lost limbs and internal organs are regrown. However, healing from all other sources is halved until the caster’s next full rest period.
