---
title: 3 - Energy Sink
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates
==Target:== caster, LOS 60 + 15/F’
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†
==Area:== self
==Effect:== make spell more costly
This spell is activated by the caster upon a spell within range. If that spell’s caster fails the power check, they must spend an additional 1 + 1/F EU or their spell has no effect (the units originally used to cast the spell are still used). This spell has no effect against spells cast from items, or against non-elemental spells.
