---
title: 4 - Spell Block
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†
==Area:== self
==Effect:== block minor spell
This spell activates the next time a spell specifically targeting the caster of this spell is cast on them. Roll 4d6 Counterspell.
