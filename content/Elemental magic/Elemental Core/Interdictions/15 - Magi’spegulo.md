---
title: 15 - Magi’spegulo
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster, LOS 200 + 50/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== rebound all spells  
Magi’spegulo creates a translucent, reflective shield around the caster. Whenever you are targeted by a spell within LOS 200 + 50/F’, roll 12d6 Counterspell, rebound on success.
