---
title: 9 - Greater Protection
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== 3d6 vs PWR negates
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†
==Area:== single creature
==Effect:== protect against spell
The target may activate this spell when they are a target of another spell or magic based effect. The target takes two dice off of any resistance check required by that spell.
