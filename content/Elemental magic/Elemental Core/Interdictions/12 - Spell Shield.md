---
title: 12 - Spell Shield
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F minutes†  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== shield against spells  
This spell activates the next time a spell or magic-based effect makes the caster roll a resistance check, and stays up for the next 1 + 1/F minutes. As long as this spell is up, roll 12d6 Counterspell against any new spell that affects you. If the affected spell requires experience to cast, you must spend the same amount of experience to successfully counter it, otherwise the counterspell fails.
