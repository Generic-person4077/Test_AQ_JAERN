---
title: 10 – Know Element
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 minute  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 10’ radius object  
==Effect:== learn elemental makeup  
Know Element enables the mage to discover the elemental makeup (earth, fire, air, water) of any object. Unfinessed, the spell reveals the dominant element only; fully finessed, it reveals all elements used in the object’s makeup and manufacture.
