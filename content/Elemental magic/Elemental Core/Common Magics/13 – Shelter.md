---
title: 13 – Shelter
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 120 + 30/F’
==Duration:== instantaneous
==Area:== 50 + 10/F’ radius
==Effect:== create building
The caster visualizes a structure within the given size limits as they begin to cast this spell. The magic then takes any needed trees, earth, clay, etc. within 120’ of the target and processes them into building supplies, and are used to construct a building as near to the visualization as the raw materials allow.
