---
title: 12 – Fasten
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 10 + 2/F’ radius  
==Effect:== work fastenings  
With Fasten, the caster can fasten or unfasten 1 + 1/F ropes, buckles, buttons, latches, etc. at a distance. By finessing, up to five fastenings can be worked in the area of effect. As a rule of thumb, most shields have two fastenings, and most armors have five.
