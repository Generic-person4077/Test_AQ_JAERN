---
title: 3 – Renew Object
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 30 + 15/F lb. item  
==Effect:== restore appearance  
Enables the mage to restore one item to its new/clean appearance. Clothing, dishes, books, etc. are likely targets.
