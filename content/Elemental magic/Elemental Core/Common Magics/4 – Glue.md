---
title: 4 – Glue
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== special  
==Area:== two items  
==Effect:== glue together  
This spell allows the caster to glue two surfaces of any two items together. The items cannot be separated by any physical means while this spell is in effect. Normally it lasts 4 minutes. One finesse allows it to last for one hour, two for one day, three for one week and four for one month.
