---
title: 2 – Change Temperature
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 6 + 2/F hours
==Area:== 15 + 5/F’ radius
==Effect:== set and maintain temp
Allows the caster to set and maintain any desired air temperature from 0 to 100 degrees Fahrenheit within an enclosed and moderately well insulated space without need for normal heating or cooling devices.
