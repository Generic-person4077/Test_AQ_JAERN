---
title: 15 – Greater Heal
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 100 + 20/F’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== 100 + 20/F’ radius  
==Effect:== heals all in area  
All within the area that are willing or fail the resistance check are healed 6d6 DP.
