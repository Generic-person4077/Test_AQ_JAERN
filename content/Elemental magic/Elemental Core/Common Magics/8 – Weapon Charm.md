---
title: 8 – Weapon Charm
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F’ minutes  
==Area:== one weapon  
==Effect:== enchant weapon  
Weapon Charm causes any weapon to function as if once enchanted for the duration of the spell. Using an item thus enchanted gives its bearer a +1 + 1/2F on any roll to hit and  damage (bonus). Multiple castings of this spell on a single target will stack with each other and permanent enchantments, up to +5.
