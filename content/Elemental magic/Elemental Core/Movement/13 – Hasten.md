---
title: 13 – Hasten
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates
==Target:== multitouch
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== 2 + 1/2F creatures
==Effect:== speed enhancement
Hasten increases the targets’ movement by 30’ per round and allows them an additional four simple attacks per round for the duration of the spell. Additional attacks only deal the attack's core damage.
