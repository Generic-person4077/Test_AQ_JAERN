---
title: 12 – Puppet
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== special
==Area:== 10 + 5/F’ radius
==Effect:== endow with movement
Puppet allows the caster to place all creatures in the area of the effect in a trance-like state. They then cast a movement spell rank 7 or below on themself. All those under the effects of Puppet may now move as the caster moves.
