---
title: 2 – Cushion
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== non-damaging fall  
While under the protection of Cushion, the target may fall from any height and suffer no impact damage. The speed of descent is unaffected by this spell; the impact occurs as if the laws of momentum have been temporarily suspended.
