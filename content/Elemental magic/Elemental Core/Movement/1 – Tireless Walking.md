---
title: 1 – Tireless Walking
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== walk without fatigue  
Tireless Walking uses elemental power to increase the endurance of the target’s body while walking, i.e., moving at half movement rate. While this spell is in effect, the target will not tire or weaken.
