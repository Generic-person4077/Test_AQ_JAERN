---
title: 10 – Speed
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates
==Target:== multitouch
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== 2 + 1/2F creatures
==Effect:== speed movement
Speed increases the targets’ movement by 20’ per round and allows them two additional simple attacks per round for the duration of the spell. Additional attacks only deal the attack's core damage.
