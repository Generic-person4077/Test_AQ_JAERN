---
title: 14 – Lightfoot
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== unnatural speed  
The target of Lightfoot is momentarily endowed with the ability to run at a movement rate of 500’ per round. This is equal to 225 mets per hour (approximately 85 miles per hour).
