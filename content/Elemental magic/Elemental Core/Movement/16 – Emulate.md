---
title: 16 – Emulate
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== emulate movement  
Emulate allows the caster to assume the movement ability of any creature they can see. Land, swim and fly movement rates of the creature are granted to the caster. The mage must still take precautions if movement is planned in a hostile environment (under water, through fire, etc.).
