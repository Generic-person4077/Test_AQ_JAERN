---
title: 4 – Catspaw
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== silent movement  
Catspaw empowers the target to move across potentially noisy areas (dry leaves, loose gravel, etc.) without a sound. Note that this spell will not silence speech, rustling clothing, etc.
