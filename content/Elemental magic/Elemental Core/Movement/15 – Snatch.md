---
title: 15 – Snatch
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== special  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== grab moving objects  
Snatch allows the target to grab any moving object and move with it. The object must have enough momentum to lift the target, else nothing happens (GM discretion). The target is also protected from any damage that might normally occur. Thus, an adventurer can snatch hold of a boulder flung by a siege machine, fly over a wall, and land unharmed.
