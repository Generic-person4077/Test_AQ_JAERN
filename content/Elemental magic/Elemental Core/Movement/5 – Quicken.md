---
title: 5 – Quicken
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== increase speed  
Quicken allows the caster to increase the target’s movement by 10’ per round and allows one additional simple attack each round. Additional attacks only deal the attack's core damage.
