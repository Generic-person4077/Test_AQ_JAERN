---
title: 11– Shadow Leap
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds
==Area:== caster’s shadow
==Effect:== jump through shadow
With Shadow Leap, the mage can make an instantaneous move (free action) to any point their own shadow touches. One such move can be made each round for the duration of the spell.
