---
title: 10 – Multishadow
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 2 + 1/F hours
==Area:== caster’s shadow
==Effect:== 2 + 1/F shadows
Multishadow enables the caster to create up to six (when fully finessed) duplicates of their own shadow, as if more than one directional light source were striking them from different directions. This spell is most useful in conjunction with other shadow spells.
