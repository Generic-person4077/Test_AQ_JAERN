---
title: 8 – Light Bane
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates*  
==Target:== 20 + 10/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 20 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== banish illumination  
Light Bane causes any non-magical illumination device to go dark (though its fuel is still consumed) in the area of effect. Magical illumination devices make the 4d6 vs PWR check or go dark.
