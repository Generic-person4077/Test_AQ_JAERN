---
title: 9 – Shadow Cast
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 10 minutes (or until used)  
==Area:== caster’s shadow  
==Effect:== invisible casting  
After casting this spell, the caster may use their shadow to cast the next spell they wish to use. A 3d6 vs PER will allow any enemies to notice the oddly moving shadow, if the caster’s shadow is surrounded in shadows, the check is 5d6 vs PER, as the shadow blends in with the environment.
