---
title: 12 – Shadow Mount
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 10’
==Duration:== 12 + 3/F hours
==Area:== one shadow
==Effect:== create mount
Shadow Mount allows the mage to create any type of creature for use as a mount by throwing its shadow on a surface and calling the creature forth. The shadow can be created with hand gestures, paper cutouts, etc. The created creature has the standard physical abilities of the copied creature, but will not engage in combat.
