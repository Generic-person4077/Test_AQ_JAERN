---
title: 4 – Shadow Object
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== 20’ radius shadow
==Effect:== create item
Shadow Object enables the caster to make a non-living, non-magical object from its shadow. The shadow must be motionless, and the caster must touch it to call the object’s form from its shadow. The created object is the size of the shadow.
