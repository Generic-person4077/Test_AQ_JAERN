---
title: 2 – Shadow Carry
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== caster  
==Effect:== give shadow strength  
Shadow Carry enables the caster’s natural shadow to carry any items the caster could normally carry. These items are visible while being carried.
