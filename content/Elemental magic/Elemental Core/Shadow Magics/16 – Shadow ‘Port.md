---
title: 16 – Shadow ‘Port
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== go to source  
By standing on a shadow and casting Shadow ‘Port, the mage travels instantly to the exterior or interior of the shadow’s source. The caster may choose where to appear in relation to the source, though they must know the layout of interior destinations to be completely successful. If they don’t, they must roll 4d6 vs AGI to avoid a stumbling re-entry and loss of initiative for 1d4 rounds.
