---
title: 1 – Dark Mist
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 2d6 vs PER negates*  
==Target:== LOS 60 + 15/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== 5 + 2/F’ cube  
==Effect:== obscure  
The caster creates a shadowy mist that obscure even in light conditions. In existing shadows, it is nearly invisible (3d6 vs PER to see it). People in the mist must make a 2d6 vs PER to be able to see through it, so those that fail take a blind fighting debuff. The mist is affected by wind. Any alternative to normal vision such as heat vision or Hear Light will allow the user to see through the mist.
