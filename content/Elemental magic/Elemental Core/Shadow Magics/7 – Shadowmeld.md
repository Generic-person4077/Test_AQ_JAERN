---
title: 7 – Shadowmeld
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds
==Area:== self
==Effect:== shadowmeld
The caster melds into existing shadows, and becomes practically  invisible, enemies can make a 4d6 vs PER each round to see you. The caster may move and/or cast a spell, but this reduces the PER check by 2 dice. Stealth: Move Silently, Stealth: Shadows or other specializations may be used in conjunction with this spell, but the checks are not cumulative.
