---
title: 3 – Contrast
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== 10 + 3/F’ radius
==Effect:== reveal the hidden
Contrast deepens the tone of any shadow, thus causing anything hidden or concealed therein to stand out sharply against a pure black background.
