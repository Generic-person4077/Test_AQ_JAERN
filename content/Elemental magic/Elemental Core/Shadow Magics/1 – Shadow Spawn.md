---
title: 1 – Shadow Spawn
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 40 + 10/F’
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes
==Area:== 10 + 3/F’ radius
==Effect:== make shadows
Shadow Spawn enables the caster to make pockets of shadow in the area of effect, as if the natural lighting direction had shifted to the caster’s desire.
