---
title: 10 – Lockjaw
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR negates  
==Target:== LOS 20 + 5/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single being  
==Effect:== prevent speech  
Lockjaw is used to prevent an individual from speaking or communicating. Thus orders can be disrupted and spell casting prevented unless using Silent Cast (CS). With 2 finesses the use of hands/limbs for gesturing, communicating, or spell casting is prevented as well. Fully finessed this spell makes giving or understanding orders impossible.
