---
title: 8 – Fluency
---
==Time to Cast:== 4 rounds
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== caster
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes
==Area:== self
==Effect:== understand
Fluency enables the caster to understand any spoken or written communication. They can also speak in this tongue if it is humanly possible. This encompasses all languages and even animal grunts and squeaks if such could be considered normal communication for the animals involved. Fully finessed, this spell also allows the caster to accurately record, and later recall or write down, any normal speech within hearing.
