---
title: 2 – Message
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== LOS 500 + 250/F’  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== long rage speech  
The caster can communicate with another person or creature within their line of sight by casting the spell, then whispering the message. Total privacy is not assured, as the whispered speech can be heard emanating from the target’s ear.
