---
title: 12 – Silver Tongue
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== caster  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F minutes  
==Area:== self  
==Effect:== very persuasive  
The caster laces their voice and words with magic to make them more convincing. Any Acting skill checks made involving the usage of voice or words are made with 2 dice off. This can include Acting: Deception, Acting: Persuasion, or Acting: Performance checks, and may even extend to Acting: Instrumental Music (Singing) checks if the GM deems appropriate.
