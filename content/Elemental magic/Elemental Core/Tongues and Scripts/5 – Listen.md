---
title: 5 – Listen
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs PWR*  
==Target:== LOS 200 + 50/F’  
==Duration:== 10 + 5/F minutes  
==Area:== 20 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== eavesdrop  
Listen allows the caster to manipulate sound and redirect it to themself. This will enable them to listen in on conversations and other sounds provided a medium for sound to travel through. This does not provide an understanding of its source other than what the caster can discern. If there are people within the zone, they can make a 4d6 vs PWR check to notice this eavesdropping. Fully finessed, the caster can perfectly visualize the area from the sounds alone, and even memlock the location based on sounds.
