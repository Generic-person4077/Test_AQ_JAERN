---
title: 4 – Name Tongue
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 round
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== LOS 5’ or caster
==Duration:== 4 + 2/F rounds
==Area:== one script or hearing
==Effect:== identify language
Name Tongue allows the mage to identify an unknown language or script. Script identification has no duration. For spoken languages, the speaker must remain within hearing of the caster for the duration of the spell.
