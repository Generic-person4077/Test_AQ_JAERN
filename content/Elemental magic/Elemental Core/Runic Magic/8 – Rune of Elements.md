---
title: 8 – Rune of Elements
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one weapon  
==Effect:== bonus damage  
Weapon rune. When activated, rune will grant the weapon a 1d6 elemental damage (bonus) on each hit with type depending on the primary element of the caster (earth - earth, fire - fire, air - lightning, water - cold). When activated, this rune will last for 4 + 2/F rounds and then disappears.
