---
title: 7 – Rune of Restoration
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one creature  
==Effect:== heal  
Body rune. When activated by the user this rune will instantly heal 4d6 damage points and then disappear.
