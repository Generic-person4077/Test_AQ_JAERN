---
title: 4 – Rune of Protection
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 24 hours until used
==Area:== one armor
==Effect:== damage reduction
Armor rune. The user decreases incoming physical damage by 1 + 1/4F. When activated, this rune will last for 4 + 2/F rounds and then disappears.
