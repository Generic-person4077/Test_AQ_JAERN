---
title: 9 – Rune of Elemental Protection
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one creature  
==Effect:== resistance  
Body rune. When activated this rune will grant the user one rank of Resist (Element type) with element type depending on the primary element of the caster (earth - earth, fire - fire, air - lightning, water - cold). When activated, this rune will last for 4 rounds and then disappears.
