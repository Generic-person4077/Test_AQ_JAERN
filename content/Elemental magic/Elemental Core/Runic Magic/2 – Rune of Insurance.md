---
title: 2 – Rune of Insurance
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one item  
==Effect:== protect equipment  
Weapon or Armor rune. Protected equipment cannot be damaged or destroyed while this rune is active. This includes results of critical hit, effects of other spells, etc. This rune activates when protected equipment would be damaged and then disappears.
