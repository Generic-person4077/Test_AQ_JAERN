---
title: 1 – Create Enchanted Ink
---
==Time to Cast:== 10 minutes
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 1 day
==Area:== one bottle of ink
==Effect:== enchant ink
Ordinary ink cannot withstand being filled with elemental energy, and also cannot be used to draw on many types of surfaces and be easily erased. The caster uses this spell to modify ordinary ink’s properties. Ink enchanted this way can be used for runecrafting, will last for a long time, and stick to any surface.  One bottle will provide enough ink to  last the entire day.
