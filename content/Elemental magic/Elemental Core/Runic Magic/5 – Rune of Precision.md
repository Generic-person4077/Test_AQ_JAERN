---
title: 5 – Rune of Precision
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 24 hours until used  
==Area:== one weapon  
==Effect:== increase protection  
Weapon rune. The user adds 2 + 1/F to their attack roll. When activated, this rune will last for 4 + 2/F rounds and then disappears.
