---
title: 11 – Rune of Elemental Revenge
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 minutes
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== 24 hours until used
==Area:== one armor
==Effect:== revenge
Armor rune. When activated this rune will infuse armor with power of elements. When a user is hit in melee, the rune deals 2d6 points of elemental damage to the attacker with type of damage depending on the primary element of the caster (earth - earth, fire - fire, air - lightning, water - cold). Multiple sources of damage from the same target will trigger rune only once. When activated, this rune will last for 4 + 2/F rounds and then disappears.
