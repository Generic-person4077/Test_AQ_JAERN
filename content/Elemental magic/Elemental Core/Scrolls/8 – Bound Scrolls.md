---
title: 8 – Bound Scrolls
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 minutes
==Resist Check:== none
==Target:== touch
==Duration:== permanent
==Area:== two Papyruses
==Effect:== create live chat
The caster takes two pieces of Papyrus and links them: when one is written on or erased, the text magically updates on the other, creating a means of written communication regardless of distance. This spell works across planes, unless blocked or prevented by other magical means.
