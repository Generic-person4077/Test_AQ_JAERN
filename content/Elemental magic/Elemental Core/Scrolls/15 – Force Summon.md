---
title: 15 – Force Summon
---
==Time to Cast:== 1 hour  
==Resist Check:== 6d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== forced familiar  
The caster uses this scroll to forcibly create a bond with a creature. They may then summon the creature via the Summon Familiar rules without a rate cap. The scroll will last indefinitely until it is used, and the creature will follow commands for 1 + 1/F days and can be un-summoned via standard spell dropping rules.
