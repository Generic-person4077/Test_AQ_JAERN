---
title: 2 – Light Spirits
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 minutes  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 30 + 10/F minutes  
==Area:== self, 200’ radius  
==Effect:== create light  
Upon activating this scroll, a floating light ball appears and follows the caster around for the duration. The light ball can move around in the area (which follows the caster) as the caster requests to shed light into dark areas.
