---
title: 1 – Papyrus
---
==Time to Cast:== 2 rounds  
==Resist Check:== none  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== 3 days (or until use)  
==Area:== caster’s hand  
==Effect:== create magical paper  
The caster summons magical paper to empower with elemental energy. This spell must be used to create more paper for each scroll spell they wish to cast in this group. They may also use two finesses to make this paper immune to fire or water damage, or four finesses to make scrolls immune to both.
