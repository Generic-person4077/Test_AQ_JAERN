---
title: 9 – Seal
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 minutes  
==Resist Check:== special*  
==Target:== scroll  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F days  
==Area:== 5 + 5/F’ radius  
==Effect:== seal creatures  
The caster sets a trigger condition upon making this scroll, and then places it to set the trap. Upon activation, the scroll creates a solid yellow barrier around the area and all within must make a 4d6 vs STR or fall prone. Creatures that try to leave the area must 4d6 vs WIL to be able to pass through the barrier. Creatures within are magically kept alive and will not starve. Once prone, checks to get back up are a contested STR, where the barrier rolls 6 dice.
