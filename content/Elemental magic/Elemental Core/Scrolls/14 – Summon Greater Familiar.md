---
title: 14 – Summon Greater Familiar
---
==Time to Cast:== special  
==Resist Check:== willing target  
==Target:== touch  
==Duration:== permanent  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== summon familiar  
This scroll functions identically to Summon Familiar, but without a rate cap. The creature must be willing and the scroll will last indefinitely until it is used.
