---
title: 16 – Greater Seal
---
==Time to Cast:== 15 minutes  
==Resist Check:== special*  
==Target:== location  
==Duration:== 1 + 1/F months  
==Area:== 25 + 10/F’ radius  
==Effect:== seal creatures  
This scroll functions identically to Seal, but the resist checks are 6d6 vs STR to remain  standing and 6d6 vs WIL to leave the area. Also, the barrier rolls 10 dice for the contested STR check to get up.
