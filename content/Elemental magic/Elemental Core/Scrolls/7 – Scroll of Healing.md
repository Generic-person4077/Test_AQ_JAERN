---
title: 7 – Scroll of Healing
---
==Time to Cast:== 5 minutes  
==Resist Check:== 4d6 vs WIL negates  
==Target:== LOS 10’  
==Duration:== instantaneous  
==Area:== single creature  
==Effect:== heal  
Upon activating this scroll, the target magically heals 3d6 DP, which will close any open wounds and fix basic injuries.
