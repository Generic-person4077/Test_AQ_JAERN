---
title: Restorative Cast
---
1000, PWR/4, #Mage #Priest #Psion   
You weave medical technique into your healing and abjurative spells. For each rank, increase the healing or Temp DP granted by the spell (if any) by 1d4. This bonus applies to the first instance of healing or Temp DP from the spell. If the spell has a duration, this effect can be reactivated on subsequent turns as a Fifth.
