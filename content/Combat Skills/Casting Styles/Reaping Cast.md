---
title: Reaping Cast
---
1000, PWR/4, #Mage #Priest #Psion    
You condense the spell’s energy to its limits, making the resulting effect more destructive. For each rank, increase the damage of the spell (if any) by 1d4. This bonus applies to the first instance of damage from the spell before any resistance checks. On no-resist spells, this damage is automatically halved. If the spell has a duration, this effect can be reactivated on subsequent turns as a Fifth.
